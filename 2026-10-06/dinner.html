DINNER - Tue 10/6

Spaghetti with ground chicken meat sauce + side salad TIMING - No practice tonight. Start 7:15 pm, eat 8:00. INGREDIENTS - Ground chicken, 2 lb - Onion, 1, diced - Garlic, 3 cloves, minced - Avocado oil, 1 tbsp (ground chicken is lean) - Pasta sauce, 2 jars (24 oz each) - Spaghetti, 28 oz (of the 32 oz box) - Salt for the pasta water - Salad: about 1/2 of a 24 oz salad bag, 2 tomatoes, 1 cucumber, ranch STEPS 1. Big pot of salted water on high for the pasta. 2. Large skillet, medium-high, 1 tbsp oil. Add chicken and onion, break up the meat, cook 8-10 min until no pink. Add garlic, 1 min. 3. Pour in both jars of sauce. Simmer on low 15 min, stirring now and then. 4. While sauce simmers, cook spaghetti per the box (about 10 min). Drain. 5. Salad: greens in a bowl, add sliced tomato and cucumber. Ranch on the side. PLATES - G15: 3 cups pasta + sauce - G11: 1 3/4 cups pasta + sauce - G6: 1 1/4 cups pasta + sauce, mild - A39: 1 cup pasta, extra sauce, large salad - C42: 2 cups pasta, double meat sauce, salad LEFTOVERS TO SAVE Plates use about 9 cups pasta.
