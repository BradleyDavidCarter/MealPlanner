DINNER - Sat 10/10

Meatloaf + mashed potatoes + corn + side salad (monthly meatloaf) TIMING - G11 game 2:00 pm. Normal dinner after. - Mix meatloaf 6:30 pm, in the oven by 6:45, eat 8:00. - Also today: cook rice for Sunday (see bottom). INGREDIENTS - 90/10 ground beef, 3 lb (thawed since Thu) - Eggs, 2 - Breadcrumbs, 1 cup - Onion, 1/2, finely diced - Ketchup for the glaze, about 1/2 cup - Salt and pepper - Russet potatoes, 3 lb (see mash note) - Butter, a few tbsp - Milk, a splash - Frozen corn, 2 bags (12 oz each) - Salad: about 1/2 of the 24 oz salad bag, ranch STEPS 1. Oven 350 F. In a big bowl mix beef, eggs, breadcrumbs, onion, about 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper with your hands. Do not overmix. 2. Shape a loaf on a foil-lined sheet pan or in a 9x13. Spread ketchup on top. 3. Bake 60-65 min, to 160 F inside. Rest 10 min, then slice. 4. Last 25-30 min: peel and cube potatoes, boil in salted water 15-20 min until fork-tender. Drain, mash with butter, milk and salt. 5. Corn: simmer with a splash of water 5-7 min (or microwave), drain. 6. Salad, ranch on the side. PLATES - G15: 2 thick slices (~8 oz), 2 cups mash, corn - G11: 1 1/2 slices, 1 1/4 cups mash, corn - G6: 1 small slice, 1 cup mash, corn - A39: 1 1/2 slices, 3/4 cup mash, large salad - C42: 2+ slices (~10 oz), 1 1/2 cups mash, corn, salad LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - A39 lunch Sun: meatloaf + salad - C42 Lundin Sun: meatloaf + mash Mash note: 3 lb potatoes is about 6 cups mashed, but plates use 6 1/2 cups plus C42's Sunday mash. Add the 1.5 lb extra potatoes from SL1 if you still have them. ALSO TODAY - Cook 1.25 lb dry rice in the rice cooker. Spread on a sheet pan to cool, then cover and refrigerate for Sunday fried rice. THAW TONIGHT - Burger patties (2 gallon bags, 8 patties): freezer to fridge for Mon.
