DINNER - Sun 10/11

Chicken fried rice with peas and carrots + cucumber salad TIMING - No practice. Start dicing 7:00 pm, skillet by 7:20, eat 8:00. INGREDIENTS - Chicken thighs, 2.5 lb, diced 1/2 inch (cook the whole tray; extra = leftovers) - Rice, 1.25 lb dry, cooked Sat and chilled - Eggs, 6 - Peas and carrots, 2 bags (12 oz each), frozen - Onion, 1, diced - Garlic paste, about 1 tbsp (on hand) - Soy sauce, about 1/3 cup - Avocado oil, about 3 tbsp - Cucumber salad: 2 cucumbers, a splash of vinegar, salt and pepper STEPS 1. Biggest skillet, medium-high, 1 tbsp oil. Cook chicken with a pinch of salt 7-8 min until cooked through (165 F). Move to a bowl. 2. 1 tbsp oil. Onion 3 min, garlic paste 30 sec, then frozen peas and carrots 3-4 min. 3. Push veg to the side. Beat eggs, scramble in the open space 1-2 min. 4. Add cold rice and the last tbsp oil. Break up clumps and fry 5 min, stirring, until hot. 5. Chicken back in. Add soy sauce, toss 2 min, taste. Too full? Do steps 4-5 in 2 batches. 6. Cucumber salad: slice cucumbers thin, toss with vinegar, salt and pepper. Chill until dinner. PLATES - G15: 3 cups fried rice, cucumber salad - G11: 2 cups, cucumber salad - G6: 1 1/2 cups, cucumber salad - A39: 1 1/4 cups, extra chicken, cucumber salad - C42: 2 1/2 cups, extra chicken and egg, cucumber salad LEFTOVERS TO SAVE Plates use about 10 1/4 cups. Save the rest for Mon: - Kids lunch: leftover fried rice - A39 lunch: small fried rice + salad - C42 Lundin: fried rice + a fresh egg THAW TONIGHT - Nothing.
