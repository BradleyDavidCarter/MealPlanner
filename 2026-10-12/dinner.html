DINNER - Mon 10/12

George Foreman cheeseburgers + oven potato wedges + side salad TIMING - SL3 Walmart trip was 11:30 AM-12:30 PM (see morning card). G11 practice 6-8 pm. - The Foreman fits only 2-3 patties at a time. Oven on 6:45 pm, wedges in 6:55, burgers 7:25, eat 8:00. INGREDIENTS - 8 burger patties, ~5 oz each (2.5 lb, thawed since Sat) - American cheese slices, 8 - Burger buns, 8 ct - Dill pickles (on hand), ketchup - Salt and pepper - Russet potatoes, 3 lb, cut into wedges - Avocado oil, about 2 tbsp - Salad: about 1/2 of a 24 oz salad bag, tomato, cucumber, ranch STEPS 1. 6:45 pm: oven 425 F. Cut each potato into 8 wedges. Toss with oil, salt and pepper. Single layer on a sheet pan (use 2 if crowded). Roast 30 min, flip halfway. 2. 7:25 pm: wedges out of the oven; turn the oven down to 200 F and put the wedges back in to stay warm. 3. Preheat Foreman 5 min. Salt and pepper the patties. Grill 2-3 at a time, 6-7 min per batch, to 160 F (3 batches). Lay cheese on each batch and hold it in the 200 F oven, covered loosely with foil. 4. Salad, toast buns, set out pickles and ketchup. PLATES - G15: 2 patties + bun, wedges - G11: 1 burger, wedges - G6: 1/2 patty burger, small wedges - A39: 1 1/2 patties open-face, a few wedges, large salad - C42: 2 patties, 1 bun, wedges, salad LEFTOVERS TO SAVE Plates use 7 of the 8 patties. Save: - C42 Lundin Tue: 1 whole patty (G6's freed half + the spare half) + wedges + undressed salad THAW TONIGHT - Ham portion B, 1.0 lb: freezer to fridge (A39 wraps Tue, kids lunch Wed, C42 roll-ups). - Shredded cheese: if the open bag won't get used, freeze half flat in a gallon bag.
