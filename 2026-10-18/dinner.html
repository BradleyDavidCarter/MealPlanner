Grilled steaks + loaded baked potatoes + corn on the cob + salad GUEST - Mom (guest through Tue 10/20 breakfast). 6 at dinner; A39-sized plate. TIMING - No activities. Load the potatoes: butter, cheese, bacon, sour cream. Eat 8:00.
