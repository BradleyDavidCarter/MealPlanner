INGREDIENTS - Flank steak, about 2.5 lb (thawed since Tue night; top sirloin if it was cheaper) - Marinade: juice of 2 limes, 3 tbsp oil, 2 tbsp Kikkoman gluten-free soy, 2 cloves garlic, 2 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp salt - Bell peppers, 3, sliced - Onions, 2, sliced - Large flour tortillas, about 12-15 - Sour cream, salsa, shredded cheese (on hand) - Black beans, 2 cans - Instant rice (on hand) STEPS 1. Morning: whisk the marinade in a zip bag, add the steak, press out the air, fridge (up to about 10 hr). 2. 5:30 pm: slice the peppers and onions; bag and fridge. 3. 8:30 pm: 2 large skillets on high with a little oil. Pat the steak dry; sear 4-5 min per side to 130-135 F. Rest 5 min on a board. 4. Same time, second skillet: peppers and onions, 6-8 min until charred at the edges. Instant rice and the beans in the microwave. 5. 8:40 pm: slice the steak thin across the grain. Warm tortillas in a damp towel, 45 sec in the microwave. 6. 8:45 pm: build fajitas at the table. 7. Fridge leftovers within 2 hours.
