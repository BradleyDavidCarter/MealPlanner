DINNER - Thu 10/22 - G11 favorite

No thaw tonight. Shredded chicken with brown gravy over mashed potatoes + Caesar salad TIMING - No practice. Eat about 8:00 pm. - 6:30 tenders simmer, 6:45 potatoes on, 7:10 shred, 7:20 gravy, 7:30 mash, 7:45 Caesar. Eat about 8:00. PLATES - G15, G11, C42: big scoop of mash + chicken and gravy - A39: moderate scoop, large Caesar - G6: kid scoop LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Chicken, gravy and mash: Fri 10/23 lunches.
