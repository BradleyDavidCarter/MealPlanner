DINNER - Sat 10/24 - Ashleigh's pick (moved from Fri)

No thaw tonight. Crockpot chicken and vegetables with gravy over white rice (gluten-free) Recipe: What's Mom Cookin' recipe TIMING - G11 game 2:00 pm. Eat about 6:00 pm. - 12:45 pm load the crockpot before leaving for the game (10 min). LOW by 1:00 pm, about 5 hr. - 5:20 rice on. 5:40 check chicken 165 F, then gravy. Eat about 6:00. INGREDIENTS - Raw chicken tenders, about 2 lb (fridge, bought Fri 10/23 at Walmart) - Baby gold potatoes, 1 1/2 lb bag, whole (halve any big ones) - Baby carrots, 8 oz (about 1/4 of the SL5 2 lb bag) - Yellow onion, 1/2, cut in small wedges - Fresh green beans, 1 lb, ends trimmed - Butter, 4 Tbsp, sliced - Chicken broth, 1/2 cup (check the GF label) - Cornstarch 2 Tbsp + cold water 2 Tbsp - Seasoning: GF chicken seasoning, paprika, black pepper on the chicken; roasted garlic seasoning (or garlic powder + salt) on the potatoes, carrots and onion; Mrs. Dash Garlic & Herb on the green beans. Check every label for gluten. - White rice, about 2 cups dry (on hand) STEPS 1. 12:45: season the tenders on both sides with chicken seasoning, paprika and pepper. 2. Potatoes, carrots and onion into the 6 qt crockpot; sprinkle with roasted garlic seasoning. 3. Lay the tenders over the vegetables. Green beans on top; season with Mrs. Dash. 4. Butter slices over the green beans; pour the broth around the edges. 5. Cover, LOW by 1:00, about 5 hr. Tenders are thinner than breasts: check at about 4 1/2 hr (5:30). If the chicken is done before the potatoes, lift it out, cover it, and put it back at the end. 6. 5:20: start the rice. 7. 5:40: lift out the chicken. Stir the cornstarch into the cold water until smooth, then gently stir it into the hot liquid. Lid off, about 1-3 min until it thickens. 8. Break the chicken into chunks, return it, spoon the gravy over. Serve over rice. Eat about 6:00. PLATES - G15, C42: big scoop of rice + chicken, vegetables and gravy - G11: medium plate - A39: moderate rice, extra green beans and carrots - G6: kid plate LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Chicken, vegetables, gravy and rice: Sat 10/24 lunch (game at 2:00).
