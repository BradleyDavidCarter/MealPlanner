Ready in about 40 minutes.Stovetop white chicken tortilla soup (no tomato) + side salad + tortilla chips. TIMING (start now) - 0 min: chop onion + garlic, cut tenders into bite-size pieces. - 10 min: soup simmering. - About 35-40 min: eat. INGREDIENTS (same as the crockpot plan) - Raw chicken tenders, 2 lb (fridge), cut into 1 inch pieces - Chicken broth, 32 oz - Diced green chiles, 2 cans (4 oz) - Great Northern beans, 2 cans, drained and rinsed - Corn, 1 can drained or 2 cups frozen - Onion, 1; garlic, 3 cloves; cumin 2 tsp, chili powder 1 tsp, salt, pepper, 1 tbsp oil - Optional: 4 oz cream cheese or 1/2 cup sour cream - Tortilla chips (SL8, instead of strips), shredded cheese, sour cream - Salad: 12 oz bag, cucumber SOUP STEPS (big pot, medium-high) 1. 1 tbsp oil in a big pot. Onion 4 min until soft, garlic 30 sec. 2. Add the chicken pieces, cumin, chili powder, 1 tsp salt, pepper. Stir 5 min until no longer pink outside. 3. Pour in broth + green chiles. Bring to a boil, then simmer 12-15 min until chicken is cooked through (165°F). 4. Add beans + corn (and cream cheese if using). Simmer 5 more min. Taste for salt. 5. While it simmers: make the salad. 6. Serve with crushed tortilla chips, cheese, sour cream on top.
