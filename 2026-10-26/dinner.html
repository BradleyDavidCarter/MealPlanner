DINNER - Mon 10/26 - crockpot white chicken tortilla soup

No thaw tonight. Tenders are fresh from the Fri 10/23 Walmart trip. Crockpot white chicken tortilla soup (no tomato) + side salad + tortilla strips TIMING - School day, G11 practice. Eat about 5:30 pm. - 9:00 am load the crockpot, LOW about 8 hr (or 12:30 on HIGH about 4 hr). - 5:00 shred chicken, add beans + corn. 5:20 salad. Eat 5:30. INGREDIENTS - Raw chicken tenders, 2 lb (fridge, Walmart Fri 10/23) - Chicken broth, 32 oz - Diced green chiles, 2 cans (4 oz) - Great Northern beans, 2 cans, drained and rinsed - Corn, 1 can drained or 2 cups frozen - Onion, 1 (on hand); garlic, 3 cloves; cumin 2 tsp, chili powder 1 tsp, salt, pepper - Optional for creaminess: 4 oz cream cheese or 1/2 cup sour cream - Tortilla strips (bag), shredded cheese, sour cream for topping - Salad: 12 oz bag, cucumber STEPS 1. 9:00 am: dice onion, mince garlic, put in the crockpot. 2. Lay the tenders on top. Add green chiles, cumin, chili powder, 1 tsp salt, pepper. 3. Pour in the broth. Lid on, LOW about 8 hr (or HIGH about 4 hr). No tomatoes or salsa. 4. 5:00: pull the chicken out, shred with 2 forks, return it. 5. Stir in the beans and corn (and cream cheese if using). Lid on, HIGH 15-20 min to heat through. 6. 5:20: make the salad. Taste the soup and adjust salt. 7. 5:30: serve in bowls with tortilla strips, cheese and sour cream on top. PLATES - G15, C42: big bowl + extra tortilla strips, seconds OK - G11: medium bowl - A39: moderate bowl, large salad - G6: kid bowl, strips on the side LUNCH + AFTER SCHOOL SNACK (Mon 10/26) - Kids (G11, G6): deli ham + cheese sandwich, grapes, Goldfish - G15: lunch is deli ham + cheese sandwich (his 1 sandwich), grapes. After school snack: Walmart yogurt + banana - Ashleigh: leftover kebabs over salad - Brad (after school snack): leftover kebabs + rice LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra soup: fridge or freeze in portions (not used at lunch this week).
