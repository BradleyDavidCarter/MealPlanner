CHANGED 10/26 ~5:15 PM: the crockpot was not started. Same soup, made on the STOVETOP now. Ready in about 40 minutes.Stovetop white chicken tortilla soup (no tomato) + side salad + tortilla chips. Plus pan-cooked fish for G15 and G11. TIMING (start now) - 0 min: chop onion + garlic, cut tenders into bite-size pieces. Heat oven to 400°F for the fish. - 10 min: soup simmering. 20 min: fish in the oven. - About 35-40 min: eat. INGREDIENTS (same as the crockpot plan) - Raw chicken tenders, 2 lb (fridge), cut into 1 inch pieces - Chicken broth, 32 oz - Diced green chiles, 2 cans (4 oz) - Great Northern beans, 2 cans, drained and rinsed - Corn, 1 can drained or 2 cups frozen - Onion, 1; garlic, 3 cloves; cumin 2 tsp, chili powder 1 tsp, salt, pepper, 1 tbsp oil - Optional: 4 oz cream cheese or 1/2 cup sour cream - Tortilla chips (SL8, instead of strips), shredded cheese, sour cream - Salad: 12 oz bag, cucumber SOUP STEPS (big pot, medium-high) 1. 1 tbsp oil in a big pot. Onion 4 min until soft, garlic 30 sec. 2. Add the chicken pieces, cumin, chili powder, 1 tsp salt, pepper. Stir 5 min until no longer pink outside. 3. Pour in broth + green chiles. Bring to a boil, then simmer 12-15 min until chicken is cooked through (165°F). 4. Add beans + corn (and cream cheese if using). Simmer 5 more min. Taste for salt. 5. While it simmers: make the salad. 6. Serve with crushed tortilla chips, cheese, sour cream on top. FISH FOR G15 + G11 (1.3 lb) Note: Carter called it tuna. The SL8 receipt says SALMON FILLET SCOTTISH, 1.3 lb. Both cook the same way below. This was the fish for Tue 10/27 (C42), so Tue's dinner needs a new plan. 1. Pat dry. Cut into 2 portions. Brush with oil, salt, pepper, a squeeze of lime. 2. SALMON: sheet pan (foil), 400°F, 12-15 min, until it flakes (145°F). TUNA STEAK instead: hot skillet with oil, 2-3 min per side, then rest 2 min. 3. Serve with a scoop of rice or the salad.
