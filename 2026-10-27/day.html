INGREDIENTS - Ground turkey, 2 lb (fridge, Walmart Fri 10/23) - Egg, 1 (on hand); about 3/4 cup crushed Ritz/Club crackers (no breadcrumbs on the list) - Great Value grated parmesan: 1/2 cup in the meatballs, 1/2 cup for the pasta, extra to top - Garlic, 5 cloves (on hand); onion, 1/4 grated (on hand); 1 tsp Italian seasoning or oregano, 1 tsp salt, pepper - Pasta (spaghetti or penne), 1.5 boxes (24 oz); GF optional - Butter, 6 tbsp (on hand); 1/2 cup milk or reserved pasta water - Salmon fillet, 1.3 lb (SL8), split into 2 portions for G15 + G11; oil, salt, pepper, lime - Salad: 12 oz bag, cucumber STEPS 1. 4:30: oven to 400F. Line a big sheet pan with foil, spray it. Small second pan (foil) for the salmon. 2. Mix turkey, egg, crushed crackers, 1/2 cup parmesan, 2 cloves minced garlic, grated onion, seasoning, salt, pepper. Do not overmix. 3. Roll about 30 meatballs (golf ball size) onto the big pan. 4. 4:50: meatballs in, about 18-20 min, until 165F inside. Salmon (2 portions) on its own small pan, brushed with oil, salt, pepper, squeeze of lime, in at the same time at 400F, 12-15 min until it flakes (145F). 5. 5:00: big pot of salted water to a boil. 5:10: pasta in, cook to the box time. Save 1 cup pasta water, then drain. 6. 5:20: same pot, low heat: melt butter, cook 3 cloves minced garlic 1 min (do not brown). Add pasta, 1/2 cup parmesan, splash of milk or pasta water, toss until glossy. Salt and pepper. 7. Make the salad. 5:30: pasta in bowls, meatballs on top, parmesan to finish. No marinara or red sauce.
