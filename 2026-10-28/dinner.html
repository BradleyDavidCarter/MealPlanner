DINNER - Wed 10/28 - crockpot CHICKEN carnitas (eat before Small Group)

No thaw. Whole seasoned chicken + boneless breasts are fresh in the fridge from SL8 (H-E-B Mon 10/26). No pork: pork shoulder is off the menu until further notice.Crockpot CHICKEN carnitas (whole seasoned chicken + breasts) + rice + black beans, salsa verde, enchilada sauce, lime, avocado (no red salsa) TIMING - Small Group 6:30-8:30 PM. Eat early at 5:15 pm, or leave the crockpot on WARM and eat at ~8:45. - 9:00 am load the crockpot, LOW 7-8 hr. - 4:45 shred the chicken, start rice. 5:15 eat. INGREDIENTS - Whole seasoned chicken (SL8, H-E-B) - Boneless skinless chicken breasts (SL8), tuck around the whole chicken if they fit - Onion, 1 (on hand); garlic, 4 cloves - Cumin 2 tsp, oregano 1 tsp, pepper. Go light on salt: the whole chicken is already seasoned. - Juice of 2 limes + 1/2 cup chicken broth - Rice, 2 cups dry (on hand) - Black beans, 1 large can (SL8) - Salsa verde jar (SL8), HCF enchilada sauce mild + green (SL8), mini avocados (SL8), sour cream, shredded cheese STEPS 1. 9:00 am: slice the onion into the bottom of the crockpot, add garlic. 2. Whole chicken on top, breast side up (remove any giblet bag). Tuck the breasts around it. Sprinkle cumin, oregano, pepper. 3. Pour lime juice + broth around (not over) the chicken. Lid on, LOW 7-8 hr (165°F in the thickest part). If the whole chicken does not fit with the lid closed: roast it instead at 425°F for about 1 hr 15 min, in the oven by 3:45; breasts alone go in the crockpot. 4. 4:45: lift the chicken out, pull off and discard skin and bones. Shred all the meat with 2 forks, toss with 1/2 cup of the juices. 5. Optional crisp: spread on a sheet pan, broil 4-5 min. 6. Warm the enchilada sauces in small bowls (mild for kids, green for adults) to drizzle. Cook rice; heat beans with a pinch of cumin. 7. Slice avocado and lime. Serve as bowls with salsa verde on the side. 8. If eating late: shredded chicken back in the crockpot with its juices on WARM until ~8:45; rice on warm. PLATES - G15, C42: big bowl, seconds OK - G11: medium bowl, mild enchilada sauce - A39: bowl over greens with avocado - G6: chicken + rice + beans separate, no salsa LUNCH + AFTER SCHOOL SNACK (Wed 10/28) - Kids (school): leftover meatballs + pasta in a thermos, fruit - G15: meatball thermos (no bread) - A39: leftover meatballs over salad - G15 snack: cheese cubes + crackers - Brad (after school snack): deli ham roll-ups + cheese cubes LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Save 2 portions chicken carnitas + rice + beans for Thu lunch (A39, C42 bowls).
