No thaw tonight. Pork shoulder is fresh in the fridge from SL8 (H-E-B Mon 10/26). Crockpot pork carnitas + rice + black beans, salsa verde, lime, avocado (no red salsa) TIMING - Small Group 6:30-8:30 PM. Eat early at 5:15 pm, or leave the crockpot on WARM and eat at ~8:45. - 9:00 am load the crockpot, LOW 8-9 hr. - 4:45 shred the pork, start rice. 5:15 eat. INGREDIENTS - Pork shoulder, about 3 lb (fridge, SL8) - Onion, 1 (on hand); garlic, 4 cloves - Cumin 2 tsp, oregano 1 tsp, salt 2 tsp, pepper - Juice of 2 limes + 1/2 cup chicken broth or water - Rice, 2 cups dry (on hand) - Black beans, 2 cans (SL8) - Salsa verde jar (SL8), 2 avocados, sour cream, shredded cheese STEPS 1. 9:00 am: cut the pork into 3-4 chunks, rub with cumin, oregano, salt, pepper. 2. Onion and garlic in the crockpot, pork on top, pour in lime juice + broth. LOW 8-9 hr. 3. 4:45: pull the pork, shred with 2 forks, toss with 1/2 cup of the juices. Optional: broil on a sheet pan 4-5 min to crisp. 4. Cook rice. Heat the beans with a pinch of cumin. 5. Slice avocado and lime. Serve as bowls with salsa verde on the side. 6. If eating late: set the crockpot to WARM with the shredded pork in its juices until ~8:45; rice on warm.
