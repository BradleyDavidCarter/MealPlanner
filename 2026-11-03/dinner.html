Sirloin is fresh in the fridge from the Mon 11/2 trip.Ashleigh's crockpot pepper steak (@quotidianrecipes) over rice, made with GF soy sauce and GF beef broth TIMING - CROCKPOT NIGHT. Eat about 6:00 pm. - 11:00 am load the crockpot, LOW 6-7 hr. 5:00 rice on. 5:30 thicken with cornstarch. 6:00 eat. INGREDIENTS - Sirloin steak, 2 lb, cut in thin strips (fridge, Mon 11/2 trip) - Bell peppers, 3, sliced; onion 1, sliced; garlic 3 cloves, minced - GF beef broth, 1/2 cup (left over from the stew cartons, or open one) - Kikkoman GF soy sauce, 1/2 cup; brown sugar, 1/3 cup - Lea & Perrins Worcestershire, 1 Tbsp (US version is GF) - Ground ginger 1 tsp; black pepper 1/2 tsp - Cornstarch, 2 Tbsp + 2 Tbsp cold water - Green onions, sliced, on top - Rice, 2 cups dry STEPS 1. 11:00: steak strips, peppers, onion, garlic in the crockpot. 2. Whisk broth, GF soy, brown sugar, Worcestershire, ginger, pepper; pour over. LOW 6-7 hr. 3. 5:00: cook rice. 4. 5:30: stir the cornstarch slurry into the crockpot, lid off or on HIGH 15-20 min to thicken. 5. 6:00: over rice, green onions on top.
