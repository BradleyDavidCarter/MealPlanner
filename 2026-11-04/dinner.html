INGREDIENTS - Beef chuck roast, about 3.5 lb, cut in 1 1/2-inch cubes (fridge, Mon 11/2 trip) - GF all-purpose flour blend, about 1 cup for the dredge - Salt + black pepper (or Kent's Original only if labeled GF) - Bacon grease or oil, 3 tbsp - Garlic, 4 cloves minced - Beef broth (GF label), 6 cups: Kent's 4 cups + about 2 cups in place of the 3 cans stewed tomatoes (NO tomato) - Worcestershire (Lea & Perrins, GF label), 2 tbsp - Fresh thyme, 3 sprigs (or 1 tsp dried) - Red wine, 1 1/2 cups (Cabernet/Merlot/Pinot); cooks off over 8 hr - Carrots, 1 lb, 1 1/2-inch chunks - Russet potatoes, 1 1/2 lb (on hand, USE SOON), peeled and cut in 1 1/2-inch chunks - Sweet onion, 1 (bought Mon 11/2 in place of the pearl onions, H-E-B had none), cut in 1 1/2-inch chunks - Bay leaves, 3; Imperial spread, 2 tbsp (bought Mon 11/2 in place of butter); parsley - 1 salad bag + dressing STEPS 1. 7:30: pat the beef dry, salt + pepper, toss in the GF flour, shake off the extra. 2. Brown in batches in bacon grease, 3-4 min a side, don't crowd the pan. Into the crockpot. 3. Garlic in the drippings 1 min, then pour in the wine and scrape up the brown bits. Pour over the beef. 4. 9:00: potatoes + carrots + onion chunks on top, then broth, Worcestershire, thyme, bay leaves. NO stewed tomatoes. LOW 8 hr. 5. (No 3:00 step this week: the regular onion went in at 9:00 instead of pearl onions.) 6. 5:00: pull the 3 bay leaves, stir in the Imperial spread, salt to taste, parsley on top. 5:15 eat. 7. If eating late: crockpot to WARM until ~8:45.
