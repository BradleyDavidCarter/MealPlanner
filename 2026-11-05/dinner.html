DINNER - Thu 11/5 - sheet-pan honey-garlic chicken

APPROVED by Carter 11/2. Shopping: Mon 11/2 H-E-B #736 list.❄️ THAW TONIGHT: Chicken tenders, 2 lb (frozen from the Mon 11/2 trip): freezer to fridge (Fri white chicken chili).Sheet-pan honey-garlic chicken with roasted potatoes and green beans (GF soy sauce; no lemon) TIMING - Eat about 6:00 pm. - 5:05 oven 425 F, potatoes in. 5:20 chicken + green beans in. 6:00 eat. INGREDIENTS - Chicken breasts, about 3 lb boneless (bought Mon 11/2 at H-E-B; fresh in the fridge, or frozen Mon and thawed Wed night), halved into thin cutlets - Honey, 1/3 cup (on hand); garlic 4 cloves, minced (on hand) - Kikkoman GF soy sauce, 1/4 cup; 1 Tbsp cornstarch; olive or avocado oil - Russet potatoes, about 2 1/2 lb, 1-inch cubes - Fresh green beans, about 1 1/2 lb, trimmed - Salt, pepper, garlic powder STEPS 1. 5:05: oven 425 F. Potatoes with 2 Tbsp oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder on a sheet pan, roast 15 min. 2. Whisk honey, GF soy, garlic, cornstarch. 3. 5:20: push potatoes to the sides, cutlets in the middle, green beans with a little oil and salt on a second pan. Brush half the sauce on the chicken. 4. Roast about 20-25 min, to 165 F. Brush the rest of the sauce on for the last 5 min. 5. 6:00: eat. Spoon pan sauce over the chicken. PLATES - G15, C42: 2 cutlets + big potatoes, seconds OK - G11: same plate (GF soy, so fine) - A39: 1 cutlet + extra green beans - G6: 1 small cutlet + potatoes, beans on the side MACROS - about 560 kcal, 45 g protein, 55 g carbs, 14 g fat per adult plate (est.)
