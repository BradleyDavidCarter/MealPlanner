DINNER - Sat 11/14 - chicken tenders in GF brown gravy

No thaw tonight. Fresh tenders from Fri 11/13 Walmart. Chicken tenders simmered in gluten-free onion brown gravy, mashed potatoes, buttered green beans. Stovetop, same evening (not crockpot). TIMING - About 40 minutes start to eat. - 0:00 potatoes on to boil. 0:05 brown tenders. 0:20 gravy. 0:25 green beans. 0:40 eat. INGREDIENTS (6 eaters) - Chicken breast tenderloins, about 3 lb (both Freshness Guaranteed packs; weights not printed, so eyeball it) - Pioneer Gluten-Free Brown Gravy Mix 1.61 oz, 2 packets (UPC 041460201002, GF) - Water 3 cups + GF chicken broth 1 cup (48 oz carton), or follow packet directions for 4 cups - 1 Herb-Ox chicken bouillon cube (optional, only if the gravy tastes flat) - Yellow onion 1, sliced; garlic powder 1 tsp; salt + pepper; oil 2 tbsp - Russet potatoes about 2 lb (of the 3.81 lb bought); butter 4 tbsp; Oak Farms whole milk about 2/3 cup, warm - Fresh green beans, 1 pack ($2.24 pack, size not printed); butter 1 tbsp, salt Potatoes: Sunday cottage pie also needs a mash topping. Use about 2 lb tonight and check how many russets are left before Sunday. GF check: read the broth carton for "gluten free" (the receipt doesn't say). Everything shared is GF for A39 and G11. STEPS 1. 0:00: peel and cube potatoes (1-inch), cover with cold salted water, boil, then simmer 15-18 min until fork-tender. 2. 0:05: pat tenders dry; season with salt, pepper, garlic powder. Large skillet, medium-high, 1 tbsp oil. Brown tenders in 2 batches, 2-3 min per side (not cooked through). Set aside on a plate. 3. 0:15: 1 tbsp oil or butter in the same pan, onion 5 min until soft, scraping up the brown bits. 4. 0:20: whisk both gravy packets into 3 cups cold water + 1 cup broth until smooth. Pour into the pan, stir until it boils and thickens, about 2 min. 5. Tenders and their juices back in. Cover, low simmer 8-10 min until the thickest tender is 165°F inside (no pink). Thin with a splash of broth if too thick. 6. 0:25: trim green beans; boil or steam 6-8 min until bright and tender. Drain, toss with butter and salt. 7. 0:35: drain potatoes, mash with butter and warm milk, salt and pepper. 0:40 eat. PLATES (approx.) - Haley (guest, G15-size). 6 at dinner tonight. No foods to avoid. - G15, Haley, C42: 3 tenders (about 6-7 oz cooked), 1 cup mash, 1 cup beans, gravy; seconds OK - A39: 2-3 tenders, 3/4 cup mash, extra beans (all GF) - G11: 2 tenders, 3/4 cup mash, beans (all GF) - G6: 1-2 tenders cut up, 1/2 cup mash, 1/2 cup beans MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size plate: about 550-650 kcal, 50-55 g protein (from typical USDA values for chicken breast, mashed potatoes with milk and butter, and green beans; gravy is a small add). Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra tenders + gravy keep 3-4 days in the fridge. - Leftover potatoes: save for Sunday cottage pie topping.
