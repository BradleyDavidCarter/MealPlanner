DINNER - Sun 11/15 - beef cottage pie

❄️ THAW TONIGHT (only if frozen): Monday 11/16 hamburger steaks, about 2 to 2.25 lb ground beef, freezer to fridge. Skip this if Monday's trip is before dinner and you will buy that beef fresh. Tonight's cottage-pie brick stays in the fridge and gets cooked tonight. Beef cottage pie (beef, not lamb) with a mashed-potato topping, peas and carrots, and a side salad. Oven and stovetop. Gluten-free broth and cornstarch. No tomato paste, no ketchup, no green chiles, no flour. TIMING - Eat about 5:45 pm. Haley's last dinner with us. - 4:40 pm start. 5:10 filling topped. 5:15 in the oven. 5:45 eat. INGREDIENTS (6 eaters) - Ground beef sirloin 90/10, the 2.25 lb brick from Fri 11/13 (fridge, not frozen) - Yellow onion 1, diced; garlic 2 cloves, minced - Frozen peas and carrots, 1 bag (12 oz Great Value steamable) - Great Value gluten-free beef broth, 1 1/4 cups - Cornstarch 1 1/2 tbsp stirred into 2 tbsp cold broth - Lea & Perrins Worcestershire, 1 tbsp (the US bottle is gluten-free; read the label) - Salt 1 1/2 tsp, pepper 1 tsp, dried thyme 1 tsp, oil 1 tbsp - Potato topping: leftover Saturday mash first. That card used about 2 lb of a 3.81 lb bag. Target about 6 cups. If the leftover is short, peel more russets (a second 5 lb bag was on the Fri list) and boil them. - Butter 2 tbsp and Oak Farms whole milk 1/3 cup, only if you are mashing more potatoes - Side salad: Marketside Classic Iceberg, 12 oz. Cucumber if it is on hand. No tomato on the shared plates. GF check: broth carton says gluten free, cornstarch only (no flour), no regular gravy mix in the pie. Shared dish is GF for A39 and G11. STEPS 1. 4:40: oven to 400 F. If you need more potatoes, peel and cube them, cover with cold salted water, boil, then simmer until fork-tender, about 15 min. 2. 4:45: large skillet, medium-high, 1 tbsp oil. Onion 3 min. Add the beef and break it up. Brown 8 min until no pink. Spoon off extra liquid if the pan is watery (90/10 should stay fairly dry). 3. 4:55: garlic, thyme, salt, pepper, and the peas and carrots. Stir 1 min. 4. Pour in 1 1/4 cups GF beef broth and the Worcestershire. Stir the cornstarch into 2 tbsp cold broth, then stir that slurry in. Simmer 3-4 min until the filling is moist and holds a spoon, not soupy. No tomato paste and no ketchup. 5. 5:10: spread the filling in a 9x13. Warm the leftover mash (and any new mash with butter and milk) and spread it on top, about 1/2 inch. Rough the surface with a fork so it browns. 6. 5:15: bake at 400 F about 25 min, until the edges bubble and the top has brown spots. 7. 5:35: salad in a bowl, undressed or with whatever dressing is already in the house. No fresh tomato. 5:45 eat. PLATES - Haley (guest, G15-size). 6 at dinner tonight. This is her last night. No foods to avoid. - G15, Haley, C42: a big wedge, seconds OK - A39: a moderate wedge and extra salad (all GF) - G11: a medium wedge (all GF) - G6: a kid wedge; peas and carrots on the side if he picks them out MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size wedge: about 620 kcal, 34 g protein, 42 g carbs, 24 g fat. Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra pie keeps 3-4 days in the fridge. - Haley is gone Monday (5 eaters). Monday lunch is sandwiches, not this pie. Do not also use the pie as snacks.
