PANCAKES (makes about 18-20, 4-inch) - Bob's Red Mill GF old-fashioned rolled oats, about 4 1/2 cups, blended into about 4 cups oat flour (or use the household GF flour) - Baking powder 2 tbsp (no baking soda needed); salt 1 tsp; sugar 2 tbsp; cinnamon 1 tsp - Eggs 4; whole milk 2 1/2 cups; butter 4 tbsp melted (plus more for the pan); vanilla 2 tsp - Optional pumpkin: whisk in 1 cup Libby's 100% pumpkin + 1 tsp more cinnamon + 1/4 cup more milk. Baking soda was not bought Friday. This version uses baking powder only, so you don't need it. Check the baking powder label says gluten-free. WHAT TO MAKE / PREP 1. Blend oats in the blender 30-60 sec to a fine flour. Whisk with baking powder, salt, sugar, cinnamon. 2. Whisk eggs, milk, melted butter, vanilla (and pumpkin if using). Stir into dry just until combined. 3. Rest 10 min. Oat batter thickens; add a splash of milk if it won't spread. 4. Griddle or skillet, medium heat, lightly buttered. 1/4 cup batter each, 2-3 min until bubbles set at the edges, flip, 1-2 min more. 5. Keep warm in a 200°F oven. Rinse, hull, slice strawberries. Spoon out yogurt. About 35 minutes start to eat.
