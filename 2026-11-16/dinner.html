DINNER - Mon 11/16 - hamburger steaks with onion gravy

No thaw tonight. Keep Tuesday's chicken tenders in the fridge from today's trip and cook them tomorrow. Do not freeze them. Ashleigh's Hamburger Steaks with Onion Gravy and Mashed Potatoes. Recipe reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/Ddy8KqCBEDG/ Shared dish is gluten-free. No canned French onion soup and no onion-soup mix (those contain wheat). Extra caramelized onion plus gluten-free beef broth stand in for the soup. Pioneer gluten-free brown gravy mix. Lea & Perrins (US) is gluten-free; read the label. Haley is gone. 5 at dinner. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 4:45 pm start. 5:30 eat. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Ground beef, 2.25 lb (90/10 is fine; the reel uses 85/15). Shape 6 oval steaks: two about 7 oz (G15, C42), two about 6 oz (A39, G11), two about 5 oz (G6 and a second piece). - Onion powder, garlic powder, black pepper, and ground mustard to taste (skip the mustard if the jar is not there). Salt on the meat is optional; the gravy is salty. - Yellow onions, 2 large, sliced thin - Pioneer Gluten-Free Brown Gravy Mix, 2 packets (1.61 oz each) - Great Value gluten-free beef broth, 2 1/2 cups, plus 1/2 cup water - Lea & Perrins Worcestershire, 1 to 2 tbsp - Optional: 1 HERB-OX gluten-free beef bouillon cube, only if the gravy tastes flat - Russet potatoes, 5 medium (about 2.5 lb); butter 6 tbsp; milk 3/4 cup; splash of cream if it is open; salt 1 1/2 tsp; pepper 1 tsp GF check: no French onion soup, no flour, no regular gravy mix. Broth carton and both gravy packets say gluten free. STEPS 1. 4:45: peel and quarter the potatoes. Cover with cold salted water, boil, then simmer until fork-tender, about 15 min. 2. 4:50: shape the steaks. Season both sides with onion powder, garlic powder, pepper, and ground mustard. 3. 4:55: skillet, medium. Cook the steaks about 5-6 min per side, to 160 F inside. Set them on a plate. Leave some fat in the pan. If the pan is dry (90/10 often is), add 1 tbsp oil. 4. 5:07: both onions into the pan. Cook about 10 min, until soft and golden, scraping up the browned bits. This extra onion replaces the soup. 5. 5:17: whisk both gravy packets into the cold broth and water until smooth. Pour in. Add the Worcestershire. Stir until it boils and thickens, about 2 min. If it is too thick, splash in broth. Taste; add the bouillon cube only if it tastes flat. 6. Steaks and their juices back in. Spoon the gravy over them. Simmer 4-5 min. 7. 5:25: drain the potatoes. Mash with butter, milk, cream if you have it, salt, and pepper. 8. 5:30: mash on the plate, steak on top, gravy over both. PLATES - Haley is gone. 5 at dinner. - G15, C42: large steak, a big scoop of mash, extra gravy - A39: medium steak, mash (all GF) - G11: medium steak, mash (all GF) - G6: the smaller steak, mash MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size plate: about 700 kcal, 40 g protein, 45 g carbs, 32 g fat. Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra steak and gravy keep 3-4 days in the fridge. - Tuesday lunch is baked potatoes, not this beef. Do not also use it as snacks.
