DINNER - Tue 11/17 - creamy garlic chicken pasta

❄️ THAW TONIGHT (only if frozen): Chuck roast, about 3 lb, freezer to fridge for Wednesday's 9:00 AM crockpot. If the roast has been in the fridge since Monday, nothing to do. A roast keeps longer than ground beef; do not thaw it on the counter. Creamy garlic chicken tenders and broccoli over gluten-free pasta, plus a side salad. No flour. Haley is gone. 5 at dinner. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 5:00 pm start. 5:30 eat. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Chicken breast tenderloins, about 2 lb - Broccoli florets, 12 oz - Gluten-free pasta, 16 oz (read the box) - Butter 3 tbsp; garlic 4 cloves, minced - Gluten-free chicken broth, 1 cup - Heavy cream 1 cup, or whole milk 1 cup plus 1 tbsp cornstarch if there is no cream - Parmesan 1/2 cup, only if the tub says gluten-free. Skip it if the label does not. - Salt, pepper, garlic powder; oil 1 tbsp - Side salad: iceberg or mixed greens and cucumber if they are on hand. No fresh tomato on the shared plates. GF check: the pasta box, the broth carton, and the parmesan tub. No flour in the sauce. STEPS 1. 5:00: large pot of salted water on high. Pat the tenders dry. Season with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Skillet, medium-high, 1 tbsp oil. Brown the tenders 3-4 min per side, then cover and cook until 165 F, about 4 min more. Set them on a plate and cut into bite-size pieces. 2. 5:10: pasta in the boiling water. For the last 4 min, add the broccoli to the same pot (or steam it if the pot is too full). 3. 5:18: butter and garlic in the skillet, 1 min. Pour in the broth and cream (or milk plus cornstarch). Simmer 3 min until it coats a spoon. Stir in parmesan if it is gluten-free. Salt and pepper. 4. 5:25: drain pasta and broccoli, add them and the chicken to the sauce, toss. 5. Salad in a bowl. No tomato. 5:30 eat. PLATES - G15, C42: a big bowl, seconds OK - A39: a moderate bowl, extra broccoli (all GF) - G11: a medium bowl (all GF) - G6: a kid bowl, broccoli on the side if he wants MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size bowl: about 680 kcal, 42 g protein, 62 g carbs, 24 g fat. Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Pasta keeps 3 days. Wednesday lunch is pinwheels, not this pasta. - Wednesday's roast should be in the fridge tonight, thawed, for a 9:00 AM load.
