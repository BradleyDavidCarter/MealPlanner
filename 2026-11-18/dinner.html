DINNER - Wed 11/18 - crockpot beef pot roast

❄️ THAW TONIGHT: Chicken tenders, about 2.25 lb, freezer to fridge for Thursday's 9:00 AM crockpot. A Thursday daytime shop is too late for a morning start. If the tenders are already thawed in the fridge, leave them there. Crockpot beef pot roast, potatoes, carrots, and green beans. Gluten-free beef broth. No tomato, no green chiles, no seasoning packet (those often contain wheat). Haley is gone. 5 at dinner. TIMING - Load by 9:00 am, LOW about 8 hours. - Early eat 5:15 pm, before wrestling (about 5 to 7 PM). - Anyone still out for Small Group (about 6:30 to 8:30 PM): crockpot on WARM, eat about 8:45 pm. - Who eats at which time is still open. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Chuck roast, about 3 lb - Russet potatoes, about 1.5 lb, in large chunks - Carrots, 1 lb, in chunks - Yellow onion 1, cut in wedges; garlic 3 cloves - Gluten-free beef broth, 1 1/2 cups - Salt 1 1/2 tsp, pepper 1 tsp, dried thyme 1 tsp - Green beans, 1 lb, trimmed. They go in a skillet at 5:00, not in the pot all day. - Optional gravy at 5:00: 1 tbsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp cold broth GF check: broth carton says gluten free. No soup mix, no flour, no green chiles, no tomato. STEPS 1. 9:00: salt, pepper, and thyme on the roast. Onion and garlic in the crockpot, potatoes and carrots around them, roast on top, broth over. Cover, LOW. 2. 5:00: roast should be fork-tender. If it is not, give it up to 30 min on HIGH and push the early plates a little later. Steam or skillet the green beans with a little water and a pat of butter, 6-8 min. 3. If you want thicker juices, ladle 1 cup into a saucepan, stir in the cornstarch slurry, simmer 2 min, and pour it back. 4. 5:15: serve the early table. Crockpot to WARM, lid on, for anyone eating about 8:45. PLATES - G15, C42: a big plate, seconds OK - A39: a moderate plate, extra green beans (all GF) - G11: a medium plate (all GF) - G6: a kid plate, roast sliced small MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size plate: about 620 kcal, 42 g protein, 38 g carbs, 26 g fat. Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Save one portion of roast on purpose if Thursday lunch is the thermos. Cook the rice Thursday morning. If you do not save a portion, Thursday lunch is a deli chicken rice bowl. - Do not also use the roast as snacks. - Thursday's tenders need to be in the fridge tonight.
