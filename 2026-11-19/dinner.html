DINNER - Thu 11/19 - crockpot chicken and veggies with gravy

❄️ THAW TONIGHT (only if frozen): Ground beef, about 2 lb, freezer to fridge for Friday's hamburger patties. If today's trip put fresh beef in the fridge for Friday, nothing to do. Ashleigh's Crockpot Chicken and Veggies with Gravy, over white rice. Recipe reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeiP5Y8txOy/ The reel uses 4 chicken breasts, about 1.5 lb, and 1.5 lb baby potatoes. This card uses plain tenders, about 2.25 lb, and 2 lb potatoes so five plates are enough. Everything else follows that recipe card. Haley is gone. 5 at dinner. Shared dish is gluten-free. TIMING - Load by 9:00 am, LOW. - 4:00 pm: green beans in. - 5:00 pm: start the rice, shred the chicken, make the gravy. - Eat 5:30 pm. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Chicken breast tenderloins, about 2.25 lb (not thighs, not breasts) - Chicken seasoning, paprika, black pepper; 1 tbsp oil - Baby yellow potatoes, 2 lb, whole - Baby carrots, 8 oz - Yellow onion, 1/2, cut in wedges - Roasted garlic seasoning - Fresh green beans, 1 lb, trimmed. In at 4:00 pm, not at 9:00. - Mrs. Dash Garlic and Herb, if that bottle says gluten-free. If it does not, use garlic powder, dried parsley, salt, and pepper. - Butter, 4 tbsp, sliced - Gluten-free chicken broth, 1 1/2 cups (the reel says 1 cup; the video showed a 32 oz carton) - Cornstarch 2 tbsp + cold water 2 tbsp, for the gravy - White rice, 2 cups dry - Optional: hot buttered rolls. Gluten-free rolls for A39 and G11 (read the label). Do not put a regular roll on their plates. The gravy, chicken, and rice are already gluten-free, so the meal is fine with no roll. GF check: broth, cornstarch, seasonings, and any roll that goes to A39 or G11. STEPS 1. 9:00: potatoes, carrots, and onion in the crockpot. Tenders on top. Rub with oil, chicken seasoning, paprika, and pepper. Add roasted garlic seasoning, the butter, and the broth. Cover, LOW. Leave the green beans out. 2. 4:00: lay the trimmed green beans on top. Sprinkle Mrs. Dash, or the garlic-herb stand-in. Cover again. 3. 5:00: rice on (2 cups dry, rinsed). Check the thickest tender; it should be 165 F and ready to shred. If the potatoes are still firm, leave them in while you shred the chicken on a plate. 4. 5:10: stir the cornstarch into the cold water. Stir the slurry into the pot. Cover, HIGH, about 10 min, until the gravy thickens. Shredded chicken back in. 5. 5:30: rice in bowls, chicken and vegetables and gravy over the top. Gluten-free roll on the side for A39 and G11 if you are serving rolls. PLATES - G15, C42: a big bowl, seconds OK. A regular roll is fine on their plates only. - A39: a moderate bowl, extra green beans, gluten-free roll if rolls are out (all GF) - G11: a medium bowl, gluten-free roll if rolls are out (all GF) - G6: a kid bowl, beans and carrots where he can see them MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size bowl, without a roll: about 580 kcal, 40 g protein, 58 g carbs, 16 g fat. A roll is about 150 kcal more. Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Chicken and gravy keep 3-4 days. Friday lunch is lettuce wraps, not this chicken. - Optional, about 8:00 pm: bake Friday's peanut-butter oat squares (recipe on the Friday morning card) so breakfast is just yogurt and grapes. 350 F, about 20 min. - Friday's ground beef should be in the fridge tonight.
