DINNER - Fri 11/20 - hamburger patties with GF brown gravy

❄️ THAW TONIGHT (only if frozen): Chicken tenders, about 2 lb, freezer to fridge for Sat 11/21 oven tenders. If you will buy them fresh Saturday, nothing to do. Oven hamburger patties made with ground beef, gluten-free brown gravy, rice, roasted carrots, and a cucumber salad. No ketchup glaze. Haley is gone. 5 at dinner. Shared dish is gluten-free. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 4:45 pm start. 5:30 eat. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Ground beef, 2 lb. Shape 6 patties: two about 6 oz (G15, C42), two about 5.5 oz (A39, G11), one about 5 oz (seconds), one about 4 oz (G6). - Salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder - Carrots, about 1.5 lb, peeled and cut in sticks; oil 1 tbsp; salt - White rice, 1 1/2 cups dry - Pioneer Gluten-Free Brown Gravy Mix, 2 packets. Follow the packet, using water or gluten-free beef broth. - Cucumber salad: 1 cucumber, sliced; a spoon of yogurt or a splash of vinegar; salt and pepper. No tomato. GF check: gravy packets say gluten free. No ketchup on the patties. No bread crumbs unless they are gluten-free; these patties do not need a binder. STEPS 1. 4:45: oven 400 F. Toss the carrots with oil and salt on a sheet pan. In the oven, about 25-30 min. 2. Shape and season the patties. 4:55: patties on a foil-lined pan. Bake about 18-20 min, to 160 F inside. No ketchup glaze. 3. 5:00: rice on. 4. 5:15: make the gluten-free brown gravy. Patties can rest while it thickens. 5. 5:25: cucumber salad. 5:30: patty, gravy, rice, carrots, cucumber. PLATES - G15, C42: a large patty, rice, carrots, gravy, seconds OK - A39: a medium patty, rice, extra carrots (all GF) - G11: a medium patty, rice, carrots (all GF) - G6: the small patty, rice, carrots MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size plate: about 640 kcal, 36 g protein, 52 g carbs, 26 g fat. Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra patties and rice keep 3 days. - Saturday lunch is not these patties. Saturday dinner on the menu is oven chicken tenders.
