DINNER - Sat 11/21 - oven chicken tenders with GF crumb

❄️ THAW TONIGHT (only if frozen): Beef sirloin or stir-fry strips, about 1.5 lb, freezer to fridge for Sunday's beef and broccoli rice-noodle stir-fry. If you will buy that beef fresh Sunday, nothing to do. Oven chicken tenders with a gluten-free crumb coating, potato wedges, and cabbage slaw with a yogurt dressing. Plain tenders crumbed at home, not thighs, and not a breaded product from the freezer. No outdoor grill. 5 at dinner. Shared dish is gluten-free. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 4:45 pm start. 5:30 eat. About 45 minutes start to eat. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Chicken breast tenderloins, about 2 lb (the tray thawed Friday night) - Gluten-free crumb: crush about 3 cups gluten-free bread or gluten-free cereal (Rice Chex or a labeled gluten-free flake). Or use a labeled gluten-free panko if that is what you bought. - Eggs 2, beaten; optional 1/2 cup gluten-free all-purpose flour for a light dredge - Salt 1 1/2 tsp, pepper 1 tsp, garlic powder 1 tsp, paprika 1 tsp, oil 2 tbsp (some for the crumbs, some for the wedges) - Russet potatoes, about 2 lb, cut in wedges - Cabbage slaw: 1 small green cabbage, shredded (or about 14 oz shredded). Yogurt dressing: 1/2 cup plain yogurt, 1 tbsp vinegar or lemon if it is on hand, salt and pepper, a pinch of sugar or a drizzle of honey. No mayonnaise is required. No tomato. GF check: the crumb, any flour dredge, and the cereal or bread you crush must say gluten free. Do not use regular breadcrumbs, regular flour, or a wheat panko. Shared dish is GF for A39 and G11. STEPS 1. 4:45: oven 425 F. Two sheet pans. Toss the potato wedges with 1 tbsp oil, salt, and paprika. Spread on one pan. In the oven, about 30-35 min, flip once. 2. Pat the tenders dry. Set out flour (if using), beaten egg, and the seasoned gluten-free crumbs. Coat each tender: flour, egg, crumbs. Set on the second oiled or parchment-lined pan. Drizzle or spray lightly with oil. 3. 4:55: tenders in the oven, about 18-22 min, to 165 F inside. The coating should be dry and browned. Do not flip hard or the crumb falls off. 4. 5:10: shred the cabbage. Stir the yogurt dressing and toss. Keep it cold. 5. 5:30: tenders, wedges, and slaw. PLATES - G15, C42: 3-4 tenders (about 6-7 oz cooked), a big scoop of wedges, slaw; seconds OK - A39: 2-3 tenders, wedges, extra slaw (all GF) - G11: 2-3 tenders, wedges, slaw (all GF) - G6: 1-2 tenders cut up, a small scoop of wedges, slaw on the side if he prefers it that way MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size plate: about 620 kcal, 45 g protein, 48 g carbs, 22 g fat. Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra tenders keep 3 days. Sunday lunch is the white-bean cucumber salad, not these tenders. Do not also use them as snacks. - Sunday's beef needs to be in the fridge tonight if it is frozen.
