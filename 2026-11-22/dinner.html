DINNER - Sun 11/22 - beef and broccoli rice-noodle stir-fry

❄️ THAW TONIGHT (only if frozen): Chicken tenders, about 2 lb, freezer to fridge for Monday's chicken, corn, and potato chowder. If you will buy them fresh Monday, nothing to do. Beef and broccoli rice-noodle stir-fry, carrots, and a side salad. Stovetop. Gluten-free soy and rice noodles that are labeled gluten-free. No tomato sauce, no green chiles. This is a new stir-fry, not chicken fried rice. 5 at dinner. Shared dish is gluten-free. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 4:50 pm start. 5:30 eat. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Beef sirloin steak or stir-fry strips, about 1.5 lb, sliced thin across the grain. This is not ground beef and not leftover pot roast unless you chose that on purpose and still have enough. - Fresh broccoli, about 1.5 lb, cut into florets - Carrots, about 1/2 lb, sliced thin - Yellow onion 1, sliced (use one from the extra onions already at home if they are still good) - Garlic 3 cloves, minced; optional fresh ginger, about 1 tsp, if it is in the house - Thai Kitchen gluten-free stir-fry rice noodles, 14 oz box (or another brand that says gluten-free). Follow the box; 14 oz is enough for five. - Kikkoman gluten-free soy sauce, 1/3 cup (green-label). Do not use regular soy. Optional: 1 tbsp honey, pepper, a splash of gluten-free beef broth if the pan is dry. - Oil 2 tbsp - Side salad: Marketside Classic Iceberg, 12 oz. Cucumber if one is left. No tomato on the shared plates. GF check: rice noodles and soy both say gluten free. No regular soy, no wheat noodles, no taco or stir-fry sauce packet unless the label is gluten-free. Shared dish is GF for A39 and G11. STEPS 1. 4:50: start the rice noodles exactly as the box says (many brands soak in hot water 8-10 min, then drain; do not over-soak or they turn mushy). 2. Slice the beef thin if it is not already strips. Pat dry and season with pepper. Cut the broccoli and carrots. 3. 5:00: large skillet or pot, high heat, 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the beef in 2 batches, 2-3 min, until just browned. Set it on a plate. Do not crowd the pan. 4. 5:08: 1 tbsp oil, onion 2 min, then carrots and broccoli 4-5 min, with a splash of water and the lid on so the broccoli turns bright and tender-crisp. Add the garlic (and ginger) for the last 30 sec. 5. 5:18: beef back in. Pour in the gluten-free soy and honey if using. Toss 1 min. Add the drained noodles and toss until coated and hot, 1-2 min. Splash in broth or water if it sticks. No tomato sauce. 6. 5:25: salad in a bowl, no fresh tomato. 5:30 eat. PLATES - G15, C42: a big bowl, extra beef, seconds OK - A39: a moderate bowl, extra broccoli (all GF) - G11: a medium bowl (all GF) - G6: a smaller bowl; cut the noodles short MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size bowl: about 650 kcal, 38 g protein, 62 g carbs, 22 g fat. Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra stir-fry keeps 2-3 days. Monday lunch is a deli chicken rice bowl, not this beef. Do not also use it as snacks. - Monday's tenders need to be in the fridge tonight if they are frozen.
