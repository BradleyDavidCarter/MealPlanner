DINNER - Mon 11/23 - chicken, corn, and potato chowder

No thaw tonight. This is the last dinner on the Cycle 5 menu. Nothing is set to thaw for a later cycle on this card. Chicken tender, corn, and potato chowder, a side salad, and corn tortilla crisps. Stovetop. Gluten-free broth and thickener. No tomato and no green chiles. 5 at dinner. Shared dish is gluten-free. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 4:45 pm start. 5:30 eat. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Chicken breast tenderloins, about 2 lb, cut into bite-size pieces - Russet potatoes, about 1.5 lb, peeled and diced (from the Thursday 5 lb bag if it is still on the counter) - Frozen sweet corn, 12 oz bag, or one 15.25 oz can of Great Value sweet whole-kernel corn if the frozen bag is missing - Yellow onion 1, diced; garlic 2 cloves - Butter 3 tbsp - Great Value gluten-free chicken broth, 4 cups (a 32 oz carton). Add a splash of water if it gets too thick. - Milk 1 1/2 cups. A splash of cream only if a carton is already open. No canned cream soup. - Cornstarch 3 tbsp stirred into 3 tbsp cold broth or milk (or the same amount of gluten-free all-purpose flour cooked in the butter). Do not use regular flour. - Salt 1 1/2 tsp, pepper 1 tsp, dried thyme 1 tsp - Corn tortilla crisps: 8-10 medium white corn tortillas, cut in wedges, 1 tbsp oil, salt. Bake on a sheet pan. - Side salad: Marketside Classic Iceberg, 12 oz. No tomato on the shared plates. GF check: broth carton says gluten free. Thickener is cornstarch or gluten-free flour only. Corn tortillas, not flour tortillas, for the crisps. Shared dish is GF for A39 and G11. STEPS 1. 4:45: oven 400 F. Toss the tortilla wedges with oil and salt. Bake about 10-12 min, until crisp, and turn once. 2. 4:45: pot, medium, melt the butter. Onion 4 min until soft. Garlic 30 sec. If you are using gluten-free flour as the thickener, stir it in now and cook 1 min. 3. 4:50: potatoes and the 4 cups gluten-free chicken broth. Salt, pepper, thyme. Simmer 10 min. 4. 5:00: add the chicken pieces and the corn. Simmer 8-10 min, until the potatoes are tender and the thickest piece of chicken is 165 F inside. 5. 5:15: if you are using cornstarch, stir the slurry in and simmer 2 min until the chowder is creamy, not pasty. Stir in the milk and warm through. Do not boil hard after the milk goes in. Taste for salt. No tomato and no chile peppers. 6. 5:25: salad, no fresh tomato. 5:30: chowder in bowls, crisps on the side. PLATES - G15, C42: a big bowl, plenty of chicken and potatoes, crisps, seconds OK - A39: a moderate bowl, extra salad (all GF) - G11: a medium bowl (all GF) - G6: a kid bowl; potatoes and chicken cut small, a few crisps MACROS - Rough estimate for a G15-size bowl with crisps: about 600 kcal, 40 g protein, 58 g carbs, 20 g fat. Not calculated from labels. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra chowder keeps 3 days. This card does not assign it to a later lunch. Cycle 5 ends after tonight.
