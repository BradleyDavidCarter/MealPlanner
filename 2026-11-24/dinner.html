DINNER - Tue 11/24 - cowboy beef stew

No thaw tonight. Wednesday's bone-in chicken thighs, 3 lb, were bought fresh Monday and should already be in the fridge for the soup. If you froze that tray, move it to the fridge now. Cowboy beef stew, with tomatoes this time, and gluten-free cornbread. Stovetop, about 2 hours after the beef is browned. 7 at dinner: household plus 2 guests (A39's parents). Each guest gets an A39-size bowl. Shared stew and cornbread are gluten-free. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 3:00 pm brown the beef. 4:15 vegetables in. 4:45 preheat for cornbread. 5:30 eat. INGREDIENTS (7 eaters) - Beef chuck roast, about 4.5 lb (2 trays from the Mon 11/23 list), cut into 1 1/2-inch cubes. It should be in the fridge, not the freezer. - Gluten-free all-purpose flour, 1 to 2 cups, for the dredge - Salt and black pepper - Oil, 3 tbsp. Brown in oil, not bacon grease. No pork. - Garlic, 4 cloves, minced - Gluten-free beef broth, 4 cups (32 oz). Tomatoes are in the pot this time, so do not add extra broth in their place. - Lea & Perrins Worcestershire, 2 tbsp. The US bottle is gluten-free; read the label. - Fresh thyme, 3 sprigs, or 1 tsp dried - Whole peeled tomatoes, 2 cans (28 oz each), with their juice. Carter approved these 2 cans on 11/23. This is not 3 cans of stewed tomatoes. - Red wine, 1 1/2 cups, only if a bottle is already in the house. The Mon 11/23 list did not buy wine. - Carrots, about 1 1/2 lb, cut into 1 1/2-inch chunks - Russet potatoes, about 2 lb, peeled and cut into 1 1/2-inch chunks - Frozen pearl onions, 1 bag (10 to 14 oz). Leave them frozen. Skip only if no bag was bought and none is in the freezer. - Bay leaves, 3 - Butter, 2 tbsp - Fresh parsley, if it is in the house, for the top - Gluten-free cornbread: 2 boxes Krusteaz Gluten Free Honey Cornbread Mix, 15 oz. Each box is one 8x8 pan and needs milk, oil, and 1 egg. Follow that box. GF check: flour, broth, Worcestershire, and both cornbread boxes say gluten-free. The older stew page says to skip tomatoes. Do not skip them tonight. Method: recipes/cowboy-beef-stew.html. STEPS 1. 3:00: pat the beef dry. Salt and pepper it, toss it in the gluten-free flour, and shake off the extra. 2. 3:10: Dutch oven, medium-high, oil. Brown the beef in batches, 3-4 min a side. Do not crowd the pot. Set the beef aside. 3. Garlic in the drippings, 1 min. If you are using the wine, pour it in and scrape up the brown bits. Return the beef and its juices. 4. Add the 4 cups gluten-free beef broth, Worcestershire, thyme, and both cans of whole peeled tomatoes. Break the tomatoes up. Add the 3 bay leaves. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook on low 1 hour. No bacon. No extra broth in place of the tomatoes. 5. 4:15: stir in the carrots, potatoes, and the still-frozen pearl onions. Cover and cook 45-60 min, until the beef is fork-tender and the potatoes and carrots hold their shape. 6. 4:45: preheat the oven to whatever the cornbread box says. Mix each box with its milk, oil, and 1 egg. Two 8x8 pans. Bake per the box so the bread is out by about 5:25. If both pans will not fit, bake them back to back and hold the finished pan covered. 7. 5:20: take out all 3 bay leaves. Stir in the 2 tbsp butter. Salt to taste. Parsley on top if you have it. 8. 5:30: stew in bowls, cornbread on the side. PLATES - G15, C42: a big bowl and a big piece of cornbread, seconds OK - A39: a moderate bowl and cornbread (all gluten-free) - A39's parents: each the same size as A39 - G11: a medium bowl and cornbread - G6: a kid bowl; beef and potatoes cut smaller MACROS - Not calculated from labels. The Cycle 6 menu does not give a nutrition figure for this plate. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra stew keeps 3 days in the fridge. Wednesday lunch is baked potatoes, not this stew. Do not also use it as snacks. - Wednesday's chicken thighs should already be in the fridge. If they are in the freezer, they need to be moved tonight.
