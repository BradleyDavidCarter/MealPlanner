DINNER - Wed 11/25 - gluten-free chicken noodle soup

No thaw tonight. Thursday is Thanksgiving. The turkeys are already thawing, and that dinner is not on this card. Homemade chicken noodle soup, gluten-free, a 1.5× batch for the 2 guests. The linked recipe is 6 servings; this batch is about 9. Light dinner before the holiday. 7 at dinner: household plus 2 guests (A39's parents). Each guest gets an A39-size bowl. The whole pot is gluten-free. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. The recipe is about 40-60 min. - 4:25 pm start. 5:30 eat. - Recipe INGREDIENTS (1.5× batch, about 9 servings) - Bone-in skin-on chicken thighs, 3 lb - Gluten-free chicken broth, 12 cups - Jovial gluten-free egg noodles, 12 oz. Two 9 oz boxes were bought; use 12 oz and keep the rest. - Onion, 1 1/2, chopped - Carrots, 4-5, chopped - Celery, 4-5 stalks, chopped. A frozen bag is what is on hand if there are no fresh stalks; use the amount of 4-5 stalks. - Garlic, 4-5 cloves, minced - Butter, 3 tbsp - Thyme, garlic powder, and onion powder. At 1.5× the linked recipe: 3/4 tsp each dried thyme, garlic powder, and onion powder. Salt: start with 3/4 tsp and add up to 1 1/2 tsp. Black pepper: about 3/8 tsp. - Bay leaves, 3 GF check: broth carton and the Jovial box both say gluten-free. No regular egg noodles. No flour. These amounts are the menu's 1.5× batch. Do not scale them again. STEPS 1. 4:25: large pot, medium, 3 tbsp butter. Onion, carrots, and celery, about 10 min, until the onion is soft. Garlic 1 min. Stir in the thyme, garlic powder, onion powder, pepper, and the first 3/4 tsp salt. 2. 4:40: add 12 cups gluten-free chicken broth, the 3 lb thighs, and 3 bay leaves. Bring to a boil, then cover partly and simmer on low-medium about 30 min, until the thickest thigh is 165 F. 3. 5:10: lift the thighs out. Add 12 oz noodles. Cook 10-12 min, until the noodles are soft. While they cook, pull off the skin, take the meat off the bones, and shred it. 4. 5:22: chicken back in. Pull the bay leaves. Taste and add the rest of the salt if it needs it. Heat 2-3 min. The soup thickens as it cools. 5. 5:30: bowls. This is a light dinner on purpose. PLATES - G15, C42: a big bowl, extra chicken, seconds OK - A39: a moderate bowl (all gluten-free) - A39's parents: each the same size as A39 - G11: a medium bowl - G6: a kid bowl; noodles cut short MACROS - Not calculated from labels. The linked recipe prints its own 6-serving figure. This card does not rescale that figure. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Soup keeps up to 4 days in the fridge. Friday lunch is Thanksgiving leftovers, not this soup. Do not also use it as snacks. - Nothing to move to the fridge tonight for Thursday.
