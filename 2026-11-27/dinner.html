DINNER - Fri 11/27 - beef smokies, cheese and crackers, queso

❄️ THAW TONIGHT: Ground beef, about 3 lb, freezer to fridge tonight for Saturday's meatloaf (household + 2 guests). That 3 lb comes from the 5 lb already on hand, which should be frozen by now. Leave the rest of the package frozen. Light and snacky, all day. Thanksgiving leftovers, plus the finger foods below. Not one plated 5:30 dinner. 7 grazing: household plus 2 guests (A39's parents). Each guest gets an A39-size share of the spread. TIMING - Put the smokies and the queso on when grazing starts, late afternoon. - Smokies in a small crockpot. Queso in a second small crockpot, or in a saucepan on the stove. - Before bed: move the Saturday meatloaf beef. INGREDIENTS - Hillshire Farm Beef Lit'l Smokies, 2 × 12 oz. The package must say BEEF. Not the regular pork-and-beef kind. - BBQ sauce and grape jelly, both already on hand, enough to coat the smokies in the small crockpot. The menu does not give a cup amount. Do not use a pork BBQ sauce. - Cheese cubes, from the cheddar block, and crackers (Ritz were on the Mon 11/23 list for this). - On The Border tortilla chips. Two bags were on that list for the chips and for snacking. - Queso: Velveeta Blanco (white queso), one 16 oz block, plus 1 can mild Rotel (diced tomatoes and green chilies, MILD). Melted together. This Rotel is the only green-chile exception on the menu. - Thanksgiving leftovers that are still within a safe fridge window. GF check: A39's food stays gluten-free. Ritz crackers are not gluten-free, and no gluten-free crackers were bought 11/23, so A39 skips the crackers. A39 eats the chips only if that bag says gluten-free. Read the smokies, BBQ sauce, jelly, and Velveeta labels. G15 has a sensitivity, not an allergy; the menu does not require a gluten-free cracker tonight. STEPS 1. Smokies: empty both beef packs into a small crockpot. Add BBQ sauce and grape jelly until the smokies are coated. Lid on, low, until they are hot. Then keep them on warm. If the package is pork-and-beef, do not serve it. 2. Queso: cube the 16 oz Velveeta Blanco. Empty 1 can mild Rotel, with the liquid in the can. Melt together in a small crockpot or over low heat on the stove, stirring, until smooth. Mild only. Do not add a hotter Rotel. 3. Cheese cubes on a plate with the crackers. Chips in a bowl next to the queso. 4. Leftovers: reheat meat and gravy until steaming. Put cold leftovers out in small bowls and put them back in the fridge. Anything that has sat out 2 hours gets thrown out, sooner if the room is warm. 5. Tonight, before bed: about 3 lb ground beef from the freezer to the fridge, on a plate, for Saturday's meatloaf. PLATES - This is a share, not a plated meat-and-potato dinner. - G15, C42: a big share of smokies, cheese, and leftovers, seconds OK - A39: an A39-size share, gluten-free only (no Ritz; chips only if the bag says gluten-free) - A39's parents: each the same amount of food as A39, including crackers if they want them - G11: a medium share - G6: a kid share; smokies cut in half if that is easier, mild queso only MACROS - Not calculated from labels. The Cycle 6 menu does not give a nutrition figure for this spread. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Refrigerate leftover smokies and queso within 2 hours. They keep 3 days. - Saturday lunch is the pasta salad, not this spread. Do not also use the smokies as snacks. - Saturday's 3 lb of ground beef needs to be in the fridge tonight.
