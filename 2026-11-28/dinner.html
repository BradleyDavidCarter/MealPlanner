DINNER - Sat 11/28 - meatloaf, mashed potatoes, corn

❄️ THAW TONIGHT: Beef sirloin or flank, about 2 lb, freezer to fridge tonight for Sunday's beef and broccoli over rice. It was bought Monday 11/23 for Sunday, so it should be in the freezer by now, not still in the Monday meat tray. Meatloaf, mashed potatoes, gluten-free brown gravy, and corn. Last dinner with the 2 guests. 7 at dinner: household plus A39's parents. Each guest gets an A39-size plate. The loaf uses a gluten-free binder so A39 can eat it and G15's sensitivity is covered. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 4:00 pm mix. 4:10 in the oven. Potatoes on by 4:35. 5:30 eat, after the loaf rests, once it is 160 F inside. INGREDIENTS (7 eaters) - Ground beef, about 3 lb (the amount for household + 2 guests). It should have thawed in the fridge since Friday night. - Binder: about 1 cup gluten-free flour, or crushed gluten-free bread. The menu allows gluten-free crumbs or gluten-free oats. Oats were not bought on 11/23, so use the flour or the crushed gluten-free bread. Use it in the whole loaf, not a separate slice. - Eggs, 2 - Onion, 1/2, finely diced (onions are on hand) - Salt, about 1 tsp, and pepper, about 1/2 tsp - Mashed potatoes, about 5 lb russets - Butter, and a splash of the heavy cream bought for this mash, or milk - Gluten-free brown gravy, from the packets on hand. Check the packet label. Mix enough for 7. Do not use all 5 packets, and do not use a regular gravy mix. - Corn: 4 cans (15.25 oz) whole kernel, the amount on the Mon 11/23 list for 7 GF check: binder and gravy packet both say gluten-free. No regular breadcrumbs. No wheat gravy. STEPS 1. 4:00: oven 350 F. Mix the 3 lb beef, 2 eggs, the gluten-free binder, onion, salt, and pepper. Do not overmix. 2. 4:10: shape one loaf on a foil-lined sheet pan or in a 9x13. Bake until the center is 160 F. A 3 lb loaf usually needs a bit over an hour. Rest 10 min, then slice. 3. 4:35: peel and cube the 5 lb potatoes. Cover with cold salted water, boil, then simmer until fork-tender, about 15-20 min. Drain. Mash with butter, a splash of cream or milk, and salt. 4. 5:10: mix the gluten-free brown gravy the way the packet says. Heat the 4 cans of corn and drain them. 5. 5:30: slice the loaf, potatoes and gravy, corn on the side. If the loaf is not 160 F yet, wait and eat when it has rested. PLATES - G15, C42: a thick slice, a big scoop of potatoes, corn, seconds OK - A39: a moderate slice and a smaller scoop of potatoes (all gluten-free) - A39's parents: each the same size as A39. This is their last dinner here. - G11: a medium slice and potatoes - G6: a kid slice, potatoes, corn MACROS - Not calculated from labels. The Cycle 6 menu does not give a nutrition figure for this plate. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra meatloaf keeps 3 days. Sunday lunch is the white-bean cucumber salad, not this loaf. Do not also use it as snacks. - Sunday's sirloin or flank, about 2 lb, needs to be in the fridge tonight.
