DINNER - Sun 11/29 - beef and broccoli over rice

❄️ THAW TONIGHT (only if frozen): Lean ground beef, 2 lb, freezer to fridge tonight for Monday's street corn beef and potato bowl. If you will buy that beef fresh on Monday's trip, nothing to do. Beef and broccoli over rice, gluten-free soy. Stovetop. 5 at dinner: G15, G11, G6, A39, and C42. Guests leave after breakfast. Lunch and dinner are household only. The shared skillet is gluten-free if the soy says gluten-free. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 4:50 pm start the rice if it needs about 30 minutes, and slice the beef. 5:30 eat. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Beef sirloin or flank, about 2 lb, sliced thin across the grain. This is the piece bought for Sunday, not the meatloaf ground beef. - Broccoli, about 2 lb, cut into florets (the Sunday share on the Mon 11/23 list) - White rice, enough for 5. Rice is on hand. The menu does not give a dry measure. - Gluten-free soy sauce. The bottle on hand was counted as 20 oz. Read the label. Do not use regular soy. - Oil, 2 tbsp - Garlic, 2 cloves, minced - Salt and pepper GF check: soy says gluten-free. Rice, beef, and broccoli are gluten-free. No wheat stir-fry sauce. No green chiles. No tomato sauce. STEPS 1. 4:50: start the rice so it is done at 5:30. Slice the beef thin if it is not already strips. Pat it dry. Cut the broccoli. 2. 5:05: large skillet, high heat, 1 tbsp oil. Stir-fry the beef in 2 batches, 2-3 min, until just browned. Set it on a plate. Do not crowd the pan. 3. 5:15: 1 tbsp oil. Broccoli 4-5 min, with a splash of water and the lid on, until bright and tender-crisp. Garlic 30 sec. 4. 5:22: beef back in. Pour in gluten-free soy, salt, and pepper. Toss 1 min. No regular soy. 5. 5:30: rice in bowls, beef and broccoli on top. PLATES - G15, C42: a big bowl, extra beef, seconds OK - A39: a moderate bowl, extra broccoli (all gluten-free) - G11: a medium bowl - G6: a smaller bowl; broccoli pieces cut small MACROS - Not calculated from labels. The Cycle 6 menu does not give a nutrition figure for this plate. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra stir-fry keeps 2-3 days. Monday lunch is deli chicken sandwiches, not this beef. Do not also use it as snacks. - If Monday's 2 lb lean ground beef is frozen, it needs to be in the fridge tonight. If Monday's trip will buy it fresh, leave the freezer shut.
