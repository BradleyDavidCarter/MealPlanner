Street corn beef and potato bowl. A39's pick. 5 at dinner: G15, G11, G6, A39, and C42. Guests are gone. Naturally gluten-free. Check the seasoning label. Amounts below are the menu's planned amounts. The Mon 11/30 shopping list is not built yet, so this card does not assume the bags are already in the kitchen. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 4:45 pm potatoes in the oven. 5:30 eat. - Reel INGREDIENTS (5 eaters, menu amounts) - Lean ground beef, 2 lb - Potatoes, 3 lb, diced, roasted crispy - Siete taco seasoning, 2 packets - Salt and pepper - Street corn: frozen corn, 24 oz, charred - Plain Greek yogurt or sour cream, 1 cup - Limes, 2 - Cotija, 4 oz - Chili powder or Tajín, 1 tsp - Cilantro, 1 bunch - A little oil so the potatoes crisp GF check: Siete packet (or whatever seasoning is in the pan) says gluten-free. Corn, potatoes, dairy, and beef are gluten-free. No flour tortillas. STEPS 1. 4:45: oven 425 F. Dice the 3 lb potatoes. Toss with a little oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on a sheet pan (two pans if crowded). Roast until crispy, about 30-35 min, turning once. 2. 5:05: large skillet, medium-high. Brown the 2 lb lean ground beef, breaking it up, until no pink. Stir in 2 packets Siete taco seasoning the way the packet says, plus salt and pepper if the packet did not already cover it. 3. 5:15: char the 24 oz corn in a hot skillet, or on a pan in the oven, until some pieces brown. Stir in 1 cup plain Greek yogurt or sour cream, the juice of 2 limes, 4 oz cotija, 1 tsp chili powder or Tajín, and chopped cilantro from 1 bunch. 4. 5:30: bowl of potatoes, beef, and street corn. PLATES - G15, C42: a big bowl, extra beef, seconds OK - A39: a moderate bowl (all gluten-free) - G11: a medium bowl - G6: a kid bowl; potatoes and corn, mild seasoning if the Tajín is too sharp MACROS - Not calculated from labels.
