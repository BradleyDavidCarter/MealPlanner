DINNER - Tue 12/1 - cheesy beef and potato bowl

❄️ THAW TONIGHT (only if frozen): the beef roast from Monday's trip, for Wednesday's crockpot. If it's in the fridge, nothing to do. High-protein cheesy beef and potato bowl. A39's pick. 5 at dinner: G15, G11, G6, A39, and C42. Naturally gluten-free. Amounts below are the menu's planned amounts. The creator listed no quantities; these are the ones the menu set. The Mon 11/30 shopping list is not built yet. TIMING - Eat about 5:30 pm. - 4:50 pm start the potatoes. 5:30 eat. - Reel INGREDIENTS (5 eaters, menu amounts) - Lean ground beef, 2 lb - Potatoes, 3 lb, diced, pan-fried - Bell peppers, 2 - Shredded cheddar, 8 oz - Avocados, 2 - Garlic powder, 1 tsp - Paprika, 1 tsp - Salt and pepper - Optional: sriracha mayo or hot sauce - Enough oil to pan-fry the potatoes GF check: nothing in this bowl needs flour, bread, or soy sauce. Read a hot-sauce bottle before it goes on A39's bowl. Skip the optional sauce if the label is not gluten-free. STEPS 1. 4:50: dice the 3 lb potatoes. Pan-fry in batches in a little oil, with salt and pepper, until crispy. Set them aside. 2. 5:10: same pan, the 2 lb lean ground beef and the 2 bell peppers, sliced or diced. Break the beef up. Cook until no pink and the peppers are soft. Stir in 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp paprika, salt, and pepper. 3. 5:22: add 8 oz shredded cheddar. Stir until it melts. Potatoes back in, just to heat. 4. 5:30: bowls. Slice 2 avocados on top. Sriracha mayo or hot sauce only if you want it, and only from a gluten-free bottle for A39. PLATES - G15, C42: a big bowl, extra beef, seconds OK - A39: a moderate bowl (all gluten-free) - G11: a medium bowl - G6: a kid bowl, without the optional hot sauce MACROS - Not calculated from labels. The Cycle 6 menu does not give a nutrition figure for this plate. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Extra bowls keep 3 days. Wednesday lunch is deli chicken lettuce wraps, not this bowl. Do not also use it as snacks. - If Wednesday's roast is frozen, move it to the fridge tonight so the morning crockpot can start. - Mix Wednesday's overnight gluten-free oat jars tonight.
