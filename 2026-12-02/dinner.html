DINNER - Wed 12/2 - crockpot beef roast

❄️ THAW TONIGHT (only if frozen): Lean ground beef, 2 lb, freezer to fridge tonight for Thursday's smashburger tacos. If you will buy that beef fresh Thursday, nothing to do. Crockpot beef roast, potatoes, and carrots. Gluten-free broth. 5 at dinner: G15, G11, G6, A39, and C42. Guests are gone. The menu does not give weights, and it says the eat time is set with the family. This card does not add a weight the menu does not have. TIMING - Load in the morning. LOW about 8 hours. Then WARM if people are not at the table yet. - The family sets the eat time. Example only: a 5:30 pm dinner means the pot goes on by about 9:30 am. - Do not put the roast in frozen. It will not be done, and the outside will sit in the danger zone too long. - Once it is fork-tender, WARM holds it with the lid on. Do not leave it on LOW unattended past the time it is done. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. INGREDIENTS (5 eaters) - Beef roast, one roast for this household. The menu does not give a pound amount. It has to be thawed before it goes in. - Potatoes, enough for 5, in large chunks - Carrots, enough for 5, in chunks - Gluten-free beef broth, enough to come about halfway up the roast - Salt and pepper GF check: the broth carton says gluten-free. No soup mix, no flour, no onion-soup packet, no green chiles, no tomato. STEPS 1. Morning: salt and pepper the roast. Potatoes and carrots in the crockpot. Roast on top. Gluten-free broth about halfway up the roast. Cover. LOW. 2. About 8 hours later the roast should be fork-tender and the vegetables soft. If the eat time is later than that, switch to WARM. 3. Slice the roast at the table. Potatoes and carrots with it. 4. Before bed, if Thursday's 2 lb lean ground beef is frozen, move it to the fridge. PLATES - G15, C42: a big plate, seconds OK - A39: a moderate plate (all gluten-free) - G11: a medium plate - G6: a kid plate, roast sliced small MACROS - Not calculated from labels. The Cycle 6 menu does not give a nutrition figure for this plate. LEFTOVERS TO SAVE - Save one portion of roast on purpose if Thursday lunch is the pot-roast-and-rice thermos. Cook the rice Thursday morning. If you do not save a portion, Thursday lunch is a deli chicken rice bowl. - Do not also use the roast as snacks. - Thursday's smashburger beef, 2 lb, needs to be in the fridge tonight if it is frozen.
