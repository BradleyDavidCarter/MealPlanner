🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • Chicken thighs, 3.5 lb (cook the whole tray; extra = leftovers)
  • Avocado oil, 1-2 tbsp
  • Garlic, 3 cloves, minced
  • Salt and pepper
  • Long-grain rice, 1.25 lb dry (on hand, about 2 3/4 cups)
  • Broccoli, 2 lb, cut into florets

🔥 STEPS
  1. Rinse rice, add water to the cooker's matching line (about 1 1/2 cups per cup of rice). Start. About 35 min.
  2. Toss thighs with oil, garlic, salt and pepper.
  3. Preheat Foreman 5 min. Grill thighs in 2-3 batches, lid closed, 8-10 min each, to 175 F inside. Hold under foil.
  4. Steam broccoli in a covered pot over 1 inch of simmering water, 6 min, until bright green.
  5. Fluff rice and plate.
