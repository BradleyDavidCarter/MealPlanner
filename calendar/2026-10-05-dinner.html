🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • Chicken thighs, 3.5 lb (cook the whole tray; extra = leftovers)
  • Avocado oil, 1-2 tbsp
  • Garlic paste, about 1 tbsp (on hand; replaces 3 cloves)
  • Salt and pepper
  • Long-grain rice, 1.25 lb dry (on hand, about 2 3/4 cups)
  • Broccoli, 2 lb, cut into florets

🔥 STEPS
  1. 6:40 pm: oven to 200 F with a sheet pan inside to hold cooked chicken. Have foil ready.
  2. Toss thighs with oil, garlic paste, salt and pepper.
  3. Preheat Foreman 5 min. Grill 2-3 thighs at a time, lid closed, 8-10 min per batch, to 165 F inside. Plan on 5-6 batches.
  4. As each batch finishes, move it to the pan in the 200 F oven, covered loosely with foil.
  5. 7:20 pm: rinse rice, add water to the cooker's matching line (about 1 1/2 cups per cup of rice). Start. About 35 min.
  6. 7:45 pm: steam broccoli in a covered pot over 1 inch of simmering water, 6 min.
  7. Fluff rice and plate.
