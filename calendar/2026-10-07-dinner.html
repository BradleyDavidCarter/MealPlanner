🌙 DINNER - Wed 10/7
Beef tacos on corn tortillas + peppers and onion + side salad | start 7:25 pm

⏰ TIMING
  • G11 practice 6-8 pm.
  • One adult drives, one cooks.
  • Start 7:25 pm (about 25 min in the skillet), eat 8:00.

🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • 90/10 ground beef, 2.5 lb (fridge portion from the Sam's roll)
  • Taco seasoning, 2 packets
  • Bell pepper, about 1 cup frozen strips (from home)
  • Onion, 1/2, sliced
  • Avocado oil, 1 tsp (for the peppers)
  • Corn tortillas, 1.5 lb (on hand; plates use 15)
  • Shredded cheddar
  • Salsa (16 oz jar)
  • Salad: about 1/2 of a 24 oz salad bag, 1 cucumber, ranch
  • Tomato, C42 only: the other 1/2 beefsteak (from Tue), diced

🔥 STEPS
  1. Large skillet, medium-high. Brown beef 8-10 min, breaking it up. Drain any fat.
  2. Add taco seasoning and water per the packets. Simmer 5 min until thick.
  3. Second skillet: oil, frozen peppers and onion, 8-10 min until soft and any water cooks off.
  4. Warm tortillas: stack in a damp paper towel, microwave 30-45 sec, or 20-30 sec per side in a dry skillet. Keep wrapped.
  5. Salad: greens, cucumber, ranch on the side. Diced tomato goes to C42 only (tacos and salad).

🍽️ PLATES
  • G15: 6 oz beef, 4 tortillas, cheese
  • G11: 4 oz beef, 3 tortillas
  • G6: 2.5 oz beef, 2 tortillas, mild (no salsa)
  • A39: 5 oz beef over salad + 2 tortillas, salsa
  • C42: 8 oz beef, 4 tortillas, cheese, peppers, diced tomato + salad with tomato
