Sheet-pan salmon + roasted potatoes + green beans | oven on 6:50 pm

  • Oven on 6:50 pm, potatoes in 7:00, eat 8:00.

🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • Salmon, 2 lb (thawed in the fridge since Wed night)
  • Russet potatoes, 2.5 lb, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • Green beans, 32 oz (bought at HEB Tue)
  • Avocado oil, about 3 tbsp
  • Salt and pepper

🔥 STEPS
  1. Oven 425 F. Line 2 sheet pans with foil.
  2. Toss potatoes with 1 1/2 tbsp oil, salt and pepper. Spread on pan 1. Roast 20 min.
  3. Meanwhile pat salmon dry, rub with oil, salt and pepper. Toss beans with the rest of the oil and a pinch of salt. Put salmon and beans on pan 2.
  4. At 20 min, stir potatoes and slide pan 2 in. Roast both 12-15 min, until salmon flakes easily (145 F) and potatoes are browned.
