🌙 DINNER - Fri 10/9
Crockpot BBQ pulled chicken sandwiches + coleslaw | crockpot on 11:00 am

⏰ TIMING
  • G11 practice 6-8 pm.
  • Crockpot on by 11:00 am-noon (low 6-7 hr).
  • Shred and make slaw at 6:00 pm.
  • Eat 8:00.

🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • Chicken breast, 3.5 lb (cook the whole tray; extra = leftovers)
  • BBQ sauce, 18 oz (on hand)
  • Onion, 1/2, sliced
  • Salt and pepper
  • Burger buns, 2 packs of 8 (second pack is for Sat/Sun leftovers)
  • Coleslaw mix, 16 oz
  • Miracle Whip (on hand), about 1/2 cup
  • Vinegar, a splash (1-2 tsp)

🔥 STEPS
  1. Late morning: onion in the crockpot, chicken on top, salt and pepper. Pour about 2/3 of the BBQ sauce over. Lid on, LOW 6-7 hr.
  2. 6:00 pm: chicken should be 165 F and fall-apart tender. Shred in the pot with 2 forks. Stir in the rest of the sauce. Set to WARM.
  3. 6:00 pm: slaw. Mix coleslaw mix, Miracle Whip, vinegar, salt and pepper. Chill until dinner.
  4. At dinner, toast buns if you like and pile on chicken.

🍽️ PLATES
  • G15: 2 sandwiches (~6 oz chicken), slaw
  • G11: 1 1/2 sandwiches, slaw
  • G6: 1 sandwich, slaw
  • A39: 5 oz chicken on 1 bun, big slaw
  • C42: 8 oz chicken, 2 buns, slaw
