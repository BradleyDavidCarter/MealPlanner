🌙 DINNER - Sat 10/10
Meatloaf + mashed potatoes + corn + side salad (monthly meatloaf) | start 6:30 pm

⏰ TIMING
  • G11 game 2:00 pm.
  • Normal dinner after.
  • Mix meatloaf 6:30 pm, in the oven by 6:45, eat 8:00.
  • Also today: cook rice for Sunday (see bottom).

🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • 90/10 ground beef, 3 lb (thawed since Thu)
  • Eggs, 2
  • Breadcrumbs, 1 cup
  • Onion, 1/2, finely diced
  • Ketchup for the glaze, about 1/2 cup
  • Salt and pepper
  • Russet potatoes, 3 lb (see mash note)
  • Butter, a few tbsp
  • Milk, a splash
  • Frozen corn, 2 bags (12 oz each)
  • Salad: about 1/2 of the 24 oz salad bag, ranch

🔥 STEPS
  1. Oven 350 F. In a big bowl mix beef, eggs, breadcrumbs, onion, about 1 tsp salt and 1/2 tsp pepper with your hands. Do not overmix.
  2. Shape a loaf on a foil-lined sheet pan or in a 9x13. Spread ketchup on top.
  3. Bake 60-65 min, to 160 F inside. Rest 10 min, then slice.
  4. Last 25-30 min: peel and cube potatoes, boil in salted water 15-20 min until fork-tender. Drain, mash with butter, milk and salt.
  5. Corn: simmer with a splash of water 5-7 min (or microwave), drain.
  6. Salad, ranch on the side.

🍽️ PLATES
  • G15: 2 thick slices (~8 oz), 2 cups mash, corn
  • G11: 1 1/2 slices, 1 1/4 cups mash, corn
  • G6: 1 small slice, 1 cup mash, corn
  • A39: 1 1/2 slices, 3/4 cup mash, large salad
  • C42: 2+ slices (~10 oz), 1 1/2 cups mash, corn, salad
