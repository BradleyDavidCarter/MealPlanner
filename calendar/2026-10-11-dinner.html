🌙 DINNER - Sun 10/11
Chicken fried rice with peas and carrots + cucumber salad | start 7:00 pm

⏰ TIMING
  • No practice.
  • Start dicing 7:00 pm, skillet by 7:20, eat 8:00.

🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • Chicken thighs, 2.5 lb, diced 1/2 inch (cook the whole tray; extra = leftovers)
  • Rice, 1.25 lb dry, cooked Sat and chilled
  • Eggs, 6
  • Peas and carrots, 2 bags (12 oz each), frozen
  • Onion, 1, diced
  • Garlic paste, about 1 tbsp (on hand)
  • Soy sauce, about 1/3 cup
  • Avocado oil, about 3 tbsp
  • Cucumber salad: 2 cucumbers, a splash of vinegar, salt and pepper

🔥 STEPS
  1. Biggest skillet, medium-high, 1 tbsp oil. Cook chicken with a pinch of salt 7-8 min until cooked through (165 F). Move to a bowl.
  2. 1 tbsp oil. Onion 3 min, garlic paste 30 sec, then frozen peas and carrots 3-4 min.
  3. Push veg to the side. Beat eggs, scramble in the open space 1-2 min.
  4. Add cold rice and the last tbsp oil. Break up clumps and fry 5 min, stirring, until hot.
  5. Chicken back in. Add soy sauce, toss 2 min, taste. Too full? Do steps 4-5 in 2 batches.
  6. Cucumber salad: slice cucumbers thin, toss with vinegar, salt and pepper. Chill until dinner.
