🌙 DINNER - Mon 10/12
George Foreman cheeseburgers + oven potato wedges + side salad | oven on 6:45 pm

⏰ TIMING
  • SL3 Walmart trip was 11:30 AM-12:30 PM (see morning card).
  • G11 practice 6-8 pm.
  • The Foreman fits only 2-3 patties at a time.
  • Oven on 6:45 pm, wedges in 6:55, burgers 7:25, eat 8:00.

🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • 8 burger patties, ~5 oz each (2.5 lb, thawed since Sat)
  • American cheese slices, 8
  • Burger buns, 8 ct
  • Dill pickles (on hand), ketchup
  • Salt and pepper
  • Russet potatoes, 3 lb, cut into wedges
  • Avocado oil, about 2 tbsp
  • Salad: about 1/2 of a 24 oz salad bag, cucumber, ranch
  • Tomato, C42 only: 2 Romas (1 tonight for his burger and salad, 1 for his Tue Lundin)

🔥 STEPS
  1. 6:45 pm: oven 425 F. Cut each potato into 8 wedges. Toss with oil, salt and pepper. Single layer on a sheet pan (use 2 if crowded). Roast 30 min, flip halfway.
  2. 7:25 pm: wedges out of the oven; turn the oven down to 200 F and put the wedges back in to stay warm.
  3. Preheat Foreman 5 min. Salt and pepper the patties. Grill 2-3 at a time, 6-7 min per batch, to 160 F (3 batches). Lay cheese on each batch and hold it in the 200 F oven, covered loosely with foil.
  4. Salad, toast buns, set out pickles and ketchup. Slice 1 Roma for C42 only.

🍽️ PLATES
  • G15: 2 patties + bun, wedges
  • G11: 1 burger, wedges
  • G6: 1/2 patty burger, small wedges
  • A39: 1 1/2 patties open-face, a few wedges, large salad
  • C42: 2 patties, 1 bun, tomato slices, wedges, salad
