🌙 DINNER - Mon 10/12
George Foreman cheeseburgers + oven potato wedges + side salad | oven on 7:15 pm

⏰ TIMING
  • SL3 Walmart trip was 11:30 AM-12:30 PM (see morning card).
  • G11 practice 6-8 pm.
  • Oven on 7:15 pm, wedges in 7:25, burgers 7:35, eat 8:00.

🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • 8 burger patties, ~5.5 oz each (2.75 lb, thawed since Sat)
  • American cheese slices, 8
  • Burger buns, 8 ct
  • Dill pickles (on hand), ketchup
  • Salt and pepper
  • Russet potatoes, 3 lb, cut into wedges
  • Avocado oil, about 2 tbsp
  • Salad: about 1/2 of a 24 oz salad bag, tomato, cucumber, ranch

🔥 STEPS
  1. Oven 425 F. Cut each potato into 8 wedges. Toss with oil, salt and pepper. Single layer on a sheet pan (use 2 if crowded). Roast 30 min, flip halfway.
  2. Preheat Foreman 5 min. Salt and pepper the patties. Grill in batches, 6-7 min per batch, to 160 F. Lay cheese on as each batch comes off so it melts.
  3. Salad, toast buns, set out pickles and ketchup.

  • G11: 1 burger, wedges
  • G6: 1/2 patty burger, small wedges
  • A39: 1 1/2 patties open-face, a few wedges, large salad
  • C42: 2 patties, 1 bun, wedges, salad
