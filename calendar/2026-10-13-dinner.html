🌙 DINNER - Tue 10/13
Sheet-pan chicken breast + baked sweet potatoes + roasted broccoli | oven on 6:45 pm

⏰ TIMING
  • No practice.
  • Oven on 6:45 pm, sweet potatoes in 7:00, eat 8:00.

🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • Chicken breast, 3.5 lb (cook the whole tray; extra = leftovers)
  • Sweet potatoes, 3 lb
  • Broccoli, 2 lb, cut into florets
  • Avocado oil, about 3 tbsp
  • Seasoning: salt, pepper, garlic paste (on hand)

🔥 STEPS
  1. Oven 425 F. Scrub sweet potatoes, poke with a fork, rub with a little oil. Bake on a foil-lined sheet pan 45 min, until soft when squeezed.
  2. 7:20: pat chicken dry, rub with oil, salt, pepper and garlic paste. Very thick breasts: slice in half lengthwise. Second sheet pan, roast 22-25 min to 165 F.
  3. 7:30: toss broccoli with oil and salt, add to the chicken pan (or a third pan). Roast the last 15 min.
  4. Rest chicken 5 min, then slice.

🍽️ PLATES
  • G15: 7 oz chicken, 1 large sweet potato, broccoli

  • G6: 2.5 oz chicken, 3/4 sweet potato, broccoli
  • A39: 6 oz chicken, 1/2 sweet potato, big broccoli
  • C42: 9 oz chicken, 1 sweet potato, broccoli
