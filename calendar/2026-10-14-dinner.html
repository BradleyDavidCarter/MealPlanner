🌙 DINNER - Wed 10/14
Crockpot beef chili + cornbread + side salad | brown beef 11:30 am

⏰ TIMING
  • G11 practice 6-8 pm.
  • Brown beef 11:30 am; crockpot on LOW by noon (about 8 hr).
  • Cornbread in the oven 7:30 pm, eat 8:00.

🥕 INGREDIENTS
  • 90/10 ground beef, 2.25 lb (thawed since Tue; 4 cans of beans carry it)
  • Chili beans, 4 cans (4th can added on SL3)
  • Diced tomatoes, 2 cans
  • Chili seasoning, 1 packet
  • Bell pepper, about 1 cup frozen, diced (from home)
  • Onion, 1, diced
  • Cornbread: 2 Jiffy boxes, 2 eggs, 2/3 cup milk
  • Shredded cheese (for A39)
  • Salad: about 1/2 of a 24 oz salad bag, cucumber, ranch
  • Tomato, C42 only: 1 Roma (last of the 3 from SL3)

🔥 STEPS
  1. 11:30 am: brown beef with the onion in a skillet 8-10 min. Drain.
  2. Crockpot: beef, beans with their liquid, tomatoes with juice, bell pepper, chili seasoning. Stir. LOW 6-8 hr.
  3. 7:30 pm: oven 400 F. Grease an 8x8 pan or muffin tin. Stir Jiffy mix, eggs and milk, rest 3 min, pour in. Bake 18 min, until golden.
  4. Salad, ranch on the side; 1 Roma diced on C42's salad only. Keep G6's bowl mild (no extra heat).
