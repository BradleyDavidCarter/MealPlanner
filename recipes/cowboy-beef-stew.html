Cowboy Beef Stew (Kent Rollins) - planned for cycle 4

Source video: The Best Cowboy Beef Stew You'll Ever Make (2 Secret Ingredients) (Cowboy Kent Rollins, YouTube, posted 10/21/2026)
Written recipe: kentrollins.com/blogs/beef/beef-stew

The 2 secret ingredients: red wine (deep, earthy richness in the broth) and frozen pearl onions (they stay whole, so you get a little onion in each spoonful).

Kent's recipe makes a big pot with leftovers, which is about right for our 5. Quantities below are his exactly. Our swaps are marked in the boxes.

TOMATO FLAG: Kent uses 3 cans (14.5 oz) of stewed tomatoes. That's off-limits at dinner because of G11's stomach. Our swap: leave the tomatoes out and use more beef broth in their place, about the same amount. (The extra broth is our change, not Kent's.) GLUTEN-FREE (Ashleigh): Kent dredges the beef in 1 to 2 cups of all-purpose flour. Our swap: use a gluten-free all-purpose flour blend for the dredge. Also check that the Worcestershire (Lea & Perrins in the US is labeled GF), the beef broth and the seasoning are labeled gluten-free. Use salt and pepper if Kent's Original isn't labeled GF. INGREDIENTS (Kent's recipe)
  • 3 to 4 lbs beef chuck roast, cut into 1½-inch cubes
  • 1 to 2 cups all-purpose flour, for dredging (GF swap: GF all-purpose blend)
  • Kent's Original seasoning, or salt and black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons bacon grease or oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 cups (32 oz) beef broth (GF label)
  • 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce (GF label)
  • 3 sprigs fresh thyme, chopped
  • 3 cans (14.5 oz) stewed tomatoes (SKIP: tomato. Use extra beef broth instead)
  • 1½ cups red wine (Cabernet, Merlot, or a Burgundy-style Pinot Noir). Secret ingredient #1
  • 1 lb carrots, peeled and cut into 1½-inch chunks
  • 1½ lbs small red potatoes, halved (quartered if larger)
  • 1 bag (10 to 14 oz) frozen pearl onions, no thawing or peeling needed. Secret ingredient #2
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, for serving
STEPS (Kent's directions)
  1. Pat the beef dry and season it with Kent's Original Seasoning, or salt and pepper, to lightly coat. Toss it in the flour (GF blend) to coat and shake off the excess.
  2. Heat the bacon grease in a Dutch oven or stew pot over medium-high heat. Brown the beef in batches until each side has a good crust, about 3 to 4 minutes. Don't overcrowd the pot. Take the beef out and set it aside.
  3. Add the garlic to the leftover drippings in the pot. Cook for 1 minute, stirring often.
  4. Stir the beef and its juices back in. Add the beef broth, Worcestershire, thyme and red wine. Leave out the stewed tomatoes and add the extra broth. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook on low for 1 hour.
  5. Stir in the carrots, red potatoes, frozen pearl onions and bay leaves. Cover and cook another 45 to 60 minutes, until the beef is fork-tender and the potatoes and carrots are soft but still holding their shape.
  6. Take out all 3 bay leaves and stir in the butter. Season to taste. Top with parsley and serve warm.

Total cook time is about 2 hours after browning. Kent says to add the vegetables after the first hour so they don't fall apart. Use chuck for the marbling (arm or round roast are leaner options).

LEFTOVERS (Kent's tip)
  • Refrigerate in shallow containers within 2 hours. Freeze portions flat in bags, and use them within 3 to 4 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and reheat until piping hot. Kent says it's even better the next day, which makes it a good lunch thermos.
